# Barnaul Jump-1 Cluster

The sample starting cluster in the [[workshop/mosh/Wardens Operations Manual\|Warden's Operations Manual]]: "a small Jump-1 cluster out in the middle of rimspace." Each system is one Jump-1 route from its neighbors.

## Systems

- Adoni: toxic, turbulent dwarf planet (B-class port)
- Aleph Gate: higher learning facility
- Waystation 61-B: SEBACO autonomous factory zone near a massive Jump-5 derelict hulk (B-class port); the Jump-3 route to the Banquo cluster leaves from here
- [[workshop/mosh/systems/Samsa System\|Samsa System]]: Samsa VI, Greta Base (C-class port)
- [[workshop/mosh/systems/Ypsilon System\|Ypsilon System]]: Ypsilon 14, SEBACO asteroid mining station (C-class port)
- Olkhova: colonial moon base (B-class port)
- Barnaul-7: liquid mini-giant with a deep sea research base (C-class port)

## Factions

- SEBACO Mining Ltd.: Ypsilon 14, Olkhova, Barnaul-7, Waystation 61-B
- Aleph Gate church: Adoni, Shedim (marked with a question mark in the source)
- Interstellar Asteroid Miner's Union: Ypsilon 14, Olkhova

## Nearby

- Banquo cluster: Jump-3 from Waystation 61-B. *Settlement Rumors* adds "Corporate has sent 3 teams to Banquo this year alone."

## Connections

- [[workshop/mosh/modules/Distress Signals\|Distress Signals]] lists a colony ship headed to Samsa VI.
