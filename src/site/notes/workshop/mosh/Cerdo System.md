# Cerdo System

RV Games' pamphlet series, mostly by Chris Airiau, collected on the "Cerdo System Pamphlets" map.

## Locations

- Cerdo's Garden: the Forever Rain and its Rainblades
- TAMA-81: station above a mining colony, with the Bernal sphere, the Company Ring and the Proxi M.T. Android Repair Workshop

## Pamphlets

- The Seed: Quarantined Settlement (not in the library)
- [[workshop/mosh/modules/The Great Crossing Heresies\|The Great Crossing Heresies]]
- [[workshop/mosh/modules/High Water Everywhere\|High Water Everywhere]]
- [[workshop/mosh/modules/Snag the Suit - Evac the Exec\|Snag the Suit - Evac the Exec]]
- [[workshop/mosh/modules/Burn the Vermin on Tama-81\|Burn the Vermin on Tama-81]]

## Factions

- Electorenzo Resources (E/R)
- The Order of the Apostle's Gate ("the Order"), followers of Cerdo the Traveler, and its Defenders of God's Gate

## Connections

- Electorenzo Resources also appears in [[workshop/mosh/modules/5th Quinquennial Interstellar Interdisciplinary Conference\|5th Quinquennial Interstellar Interdisciplinary Conference]] and *Rusted to the Core*.
- [[workshop/mosh/modules/Seen on the Void's Edge\|Seen on the Void's Edge]] has a Rainblade flee "into the Constant Clouds where they drink in the Forever Rain."
- Proxi M.T., which has a repair workshop on TAMA-81, and the Android Rights Association link *Rusted to the Core*, *Bones on the Ground*, [[workshop/mosh/modules/ConSen Android Factory Tour\|ConSen Android Factory Tour]], and [[workshop/mosh/modules/Proxi MT's USED Android Catalogue\|Proxi MT's USED Android Catalogue]].
- *They Won't Cage Us* shares Proxi M.T. and Derrivin Detention with *Bones on the Ground*.
- [[workshop/mosh/modules/Cendrillon Mercs for Hire\|Cendrillon Mercs for Hire]] calls back to Ori Moineaux, the suit from [[workshop/mosh/modules/Snag the Suit - Evac the Exec\|Snag the Suit]].
- The conference program covers Mwana II, whose jerryvines appear in [[workshop/mosh/modules/The Great Crossing Heresies\|The Great Crossing Heresies]].
