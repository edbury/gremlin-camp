# Blatteus Sector

Spider00x's (Eric Alsandor's) sector, named in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Lair of the Biomancer\|Lair of the Biomancer]] and [[workshop/mosh/modules/Miami Laser Cutter Massacre\|Miami Laser Cutter Massacre]]. The three adventures were released together as *Galaxy of Death*.

## Locations

- Calliope System: the remote tropical moon of Agamemnon ([[workshop/mosh/modules/Lair of the Biomancer\|Lair of the Biomancer]])

## Factions

- Varhasi Outer Humanist Union
- The pirates of the Shrike: their ship is in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Rane in Blood\|Rane in Blood]], and salvage from it turns up in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Lair of the Biomancer\|Lair of the Biomancer]]
- New Dawn Pharmatek
- The Kurzi Kutlar: criminal syndicate
