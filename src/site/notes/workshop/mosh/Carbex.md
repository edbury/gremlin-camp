# Carbex

No source names this sector; the label is unofficial. Tim Obermueller's adventures here share Sineto Chem Solutions. [[workshop/mosh/modules/The Burning of Carbex\|The Burning of Carbex]] and [[workshop/mosh/modules/The House Always Wins\|The House Always Wins]] also share Starpoly and the Toil & Trouble Collective.

## Locations

- Carbex: charcoal mining world, with the Angelus Fabrica refinery station in low orbit
- Sineto Deep Space Factory Station
- Thunziter system ([[workshop/mosh/modules/Hoss\|Hoss]])
- Dalmorus VIII: the city of Salnati ([[workshop/mosh/modules/The House Always Wins\|The House Always Wins]])

## Factions

- Starpoly Strategic
- Sineto Chem Solutions
- The Toil & Trouble Collective
- JEXIFLERBO Capital
- Dextro-Singleton Spacial Solutions

## Connections

- [[workshop/mosh/modules/Distress Signals\|Distress Signals]] lists a ship whose home port is the Sineto Deep Space Factory, and points to [[workshop/mosh/modules/The Burning of Carbex\|The Burning of Carbex]] for it.
- [[workshop/mosh/modules/The House Always Wins\|The House Always Wins]] points readers to [[workshop/mosh/modules/The Mole on PIRAD ONE\|The Mole on PIRAD ONE]] for Dextro-Singleton, and [[workshop/mosh/modules/Corpo Culture Killed My Dog\|Corpo Culture Killed My Dog]] covers a Dextro-Singleton strike.
