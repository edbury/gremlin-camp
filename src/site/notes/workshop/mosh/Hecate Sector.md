# Hecate Sector

Joshua Justice's setting, "this sparsely settled rim belt" ([[workshop/mosh/modules/Alternate Classes from the Hecate Sector\|Alternate Classes from the Hecate Sector]]).

## Locations

- Hecate Sector Station: Pure Transmission's orbital broadcasting facility over the ice moon Boreas
- Hecate Cassette Archive

## Factions

- Pure Transmission
- Analog Survey: radical anarchist group
- Void Musicians
- Cult of Hekate
- Neo-Reactionary Coalition

## Connections

- [[workshop/mosh/modules/Analog Survey N.1\|Analog Survey]] reviews songs titled after the Dawnseekers and the Kadaar of [[workshop/mosh/Karth-Mani\|Karth-Mani]], and [[workshop/mosh/modules/Arkady's Outpost\|Arkady's Outpost]] on Karth sells the zine.
- Found items in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Radio Free Hekate\|Radio Free Hekate]] include *Rimwise Dispatch* and [[workshop/mosh/modules/Erno Noise\|Erno Noise]].
