# Nakatomi Sector 1365

Magnum Galaxy Games' corporate sector on the Outer Rim, named in [[workshop/mosh/modules/The Smuggler's Guide to 1365\|The Smuggler's Guide to 1365]] and [[workshop/mosh/modules/Decrypted - The Book of Ghosts\|Decrypted: The Book of Ghosts]]. Nakatomi Solutions administers Hardlight Station. [[workshop/mosh/modules/Decrypted - The Book of Ghosts\|Decrypted: The Book of Ghosts]] collects paranormal reports leaked from the servers of Nakatomi's Corporate Intelligence Division.

## Locations

Ports of call from Hardlight Station, per [[workshop/mosh/modules/Dying Hard on Hardlight Station\|Dying Hard on Hardlight Station]]:

- Hardlight Station: commercial outpost at the edge of the Epsilon Eridani system, on the route between Earth and the colonized systems of the local cluster
- [[workshop/mosh/Carnath System\|Carnath System]]: TeraTek Amalgamate Mine Works, with a XeroCorp for-profit prison in orbit (Prison Station Echo, named in [[workshop/mosh/modules/The Smuggler's Guide to 1365\|The Smuggler's Guide to 1365]])
- Tau Sigma 7: Nakatomi orbital station over alleged alien ruins
- Bodhisattva IIa: Neo-Buddhist colony Siddhartha's Peace, subsidized by Straylight LLC
- Medusa Station: few ships come and go, and those that do travel in secret. [[workshop/mosh/modules/Decrypted - The Book of Ghosts\|Decrypted]] places a Nakatomi mortuary complex there and an experimental settlement on Medusa 4.
- Excelsior Cloud City on the gas giant Virago
- Champion Downs Asteroid Sports Complex
- Charybdis Debris Fields
- Sounder 4 Wormhole Research Station
- Hellmire Exclusion Zone
- Behemoth 3 Megafauna Game Reserve

Elsewhere in the sector:

- Ma'at: the Dead City of Ma'at, a wasteland of concrete dunes and ruins
- Achernar system: where the Dyson Initiative's CF Senespera vanished; the salvage job is posted on Hardlight's board
- Rigel: under blockade
