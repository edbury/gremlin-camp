# The Third Sector

Anodyne Printware's sandbox pamphlet by Ian Yusem, which stitches 12 independently created modules into one sector.

## Systems

- α-Ω System: Azure Base ([[workshop/mosh/modules/Moonbase Blues\|Moonbase Blues]]); Jump 1 to The Last Nebula, Jump 3 to the Dice System
- Dice System (high-sec corespace): The Skaana ([[workshop/mosh/modules/The Black Heart of Paradise\|The Black Heart of Paradise]]), The Dinoplex ([[workshop/mosh/modules/Dinoplex Cataclysm\|Dinoplex Cataclysm]]), Enceladus Research Facility ([[workshop/mosh/modules/Welcome to ERF\|Welcome to ERF]]); Jump 2 to Narcissus, Jump 5 to Eridani
- Narcissus System (pirate-ridden rimspace): [[workshop/mosh/modules/ALCOR Station\|ALCOR Station]], Nergal Station (Diminishing Returns), The Lima (Green Tomb)
- Eridani System: Outpost Choi ([[workshop/mosh/modules/Bloom\|Bloom]]), Shen Kuo Research Facility on Ur ([[workshop/mosh/modules/Primeval\|Primeval]]); Jump 3 to Narcissus

## Anomalies

- The Black Pyramid ([[workshop/mosh/modules/Black Pyramid\|Black Pyramid]]): wanders
- The Graveyard ([[workshop/mosh/modules/Echoes in the Graveyard\|Echoes in the Graveyard]]): wanders

The Last Nebula (The Last Nebula) is a Jump 1 destination from the α-Ω System.

[[workshop/mosh/modules/Welcome to ERF\|Welcome to ERF]] sets its facility on Saturn's moon Enceladus; the pamphlet moves it to the Dice System.

## Not in the library

- Diminishing Returns
- Green Tomb
- The Last Nebula
