# Karth-Mani

No source names this region; the label is unofficial. Karth is "a tiny mesa-studded moon on the galaxy's far edge." Mani is the tidally locked water world whose colony was founded to feed Karth.

## Locations

- Karth: Larstown, the Silver Spire, Old Seahorse Mine, Fort Vee Cee
- Mani: Leeshade, the Wet Market, Ship Palace, the Sharpe Company Compound
- Tinea Weather Station: in decaying orbit around Mani
- Sable (Outpost S-4813): Sharpe Co.'s Kadaar mine

## Factions

- Sharpe Co.: holds extraction rights on Mani and runs the blockade of Sable
- Manian Expeditionary Force
- Dawnseekers
- Land Barons of Mani
- Valley Rangers
- The Wigoy: Karth's alien natives, wiped out with few exceptions

## Connections

- [[workshop/mosh/modules/Graveyard of the Gods\|Graveyard of the Gods]] borrows Karth and the Manian Expeditionary Force alongside Prospero's Dream and Hull Breach material.
- [[workshop/mosh/modules/Distress Signals\|Distress Signals]] routes a cargo ship from Karth to Prospero's Dream, and earmarks a water shipment for the Manian Expeditionary Force on Karth.
- [[workshop/mosh/modules/The Bloodfields at Blackstar Station\|The Bloodfields at Blackstar Station]] refers to "the rimspace moon of Karth."
- [[workshop/mosh/modules/Analog Survey N.1\|Analog Survey]] ([[workshop/mosh/Hecate Sector\|Hecate Sector]]) reviews songs titled after the Dawnseekers and the Kadaar.
