# Nakatomi Sector 1365

Nakatomi Sector 1365, also called Nakatomi Corporate Sector N1365, lies in the Outer Rim, beyond the grasp of the core worlds. Beneath a thin veneer of corporate control, criminal syndicates clash with pirate fleets, nomadic scavengers and desperate operators. The sector is pockmarked with black and grey markets for those who operate beyond the reach of corporate law. Paranormal encounters are logged throughout the sector, often enough that the Nakatomi Corporate Intelligence Division keeps a standard Paranormal Event Report Form for them.

## Locations

- Hardlight Station: a small, bustling commercial outpost orbiting at the edge of the Epsilon Eridani system, and a waystation on the busy route between Earth and the colonized systems of the local cluster. Administrator Takako McClane of Nakatomi Solutions Inc. rents space to any organization looking to do business there. Its docks arrange transport to many local sector locations.
- [[workshop/mosh/Carnath System\|Carnath]]: a mining backwater at the edge of rimspace, governed by the Corpo-Triad of TeraTek Amalgamate, Falsa Robotics and XeroCorp. The TeraTek Amalgamate Mine Works there is a port of call from Hardlight Station.
- Prison Station Echo: the oldest of six near-identical prefabricated XeroCorp prisons on the small moons and asteroids orbiting Carnath.
- Tau Sigma 7: home of a Nakatomi orbital station. The planet began as a terraforming prospect, and all work halted when alleged alien ruins were uncovered on the surface.
- Bodhisattva IIa: a moon with the small Neo-Buddhist colony Siddhartha's Peace, a religious refuge and H2O farm subsidized by Straylight LLC.
- Medusa Station: few ships come and go, and those that do travel in secret. Nakatomi has a mortuary complex there.
- Medusa 4: site of a Nakatomi experimental settlement.
- Excelsior Cloud City: an exclusive resort for the sector's wealthy and famous, on the gas giant Virago.
- Champion Downs Asteroid Sports Complex: the premier entertainment destination in the sector and home of the Nakatomi Nightjars gravball team.
- Charybdis Debris Fields: the shattered remnants of a long-dead alien civilization.
- Sounder 4 Wormhole Research Station: scientists here study the phenomena that proliferate around tears in space-time.
- Hellmire Exclusion Zone: poorly manned patrols watch the perimeter, and automated beacons warn that all trespassers will be shot.
- Behemoth 3 Megafauna Game Reserve: a super Earth where people pay to hunt the massive creatures of its grasslands.
- Ma'at: a far-flung world of ancient ruins and black-market hubs, and a wasteland of ground concrete dunes and endless, nonsensical ruins half-buried in shifting sands. Landis University of Venus and Sunray Interstellar have a joint expedition team there.
- The Barge Bazaar: the most infamous of the sector's black markets, an ancient Sunray Interstellar terraforming platform found drifting dark decades ago and gutted to make a sprawling mobile market. Its ruler is known only as "The Queen".

## Factions

- [[workshop/mosh/companies/nakatomi solutions\|Nakatomi Solutions]]: administers Hardlight Station and runs the orbital station at Tau Sigma 7.
- Nakatomi Corporate Intelligence Division (NCID): led by CEO Jiro Takagi.
- decrypted: anonymous technicians on Hardlight Station who publish documents exposing Nakatomi.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/healthtek ag\|Healthtek AG]]: its advanced research module, bolted to the underbelly of Hardlight Station, is the station's most lucrative contractor.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/blackshield\|Blackshield Mercenary Company]]
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/xerocorp\|XeroCorp]]: specializes in security and military R&D on Carnath, with prisons orbiting the planet. XeroArms is its military security division.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/teratek amalgamate\|TeraTek Amalgamate]]: handles most of Carnath's mining operations.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/falsa robotics\|Falsa Robotics]]: builds and supplies work and service androids on Carnath.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/kulakov mining cooperative\|Kulakov Mining Cooperative]]: a worker-owned cooperative that excavates areas the larger corporations pass over.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/straylight computing systems\|Straylight LLC]]
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/sunray interstellar\|Sunray Interstellar]]
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/landis university\|Landis University]]
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/smoking darkness reclamations\|Smoking Darkness Reclamations]]: an outer rim salvage outfit.
- The Light of the Interstellar Way: keeps a mission on Hardlight Station.

## Connections

- Interstellar Opportunities on Hardlight Station carries a [[workshop/mosh/companies/xerocorp\|XeroCorp]] contract for night cleaners at its for-profit prison facility in orbit around [[workshop/mosh/Carnath System\|Carnath]].
- A [[workshop/mosh/companies/nakatomi solutions\|Nakatomi Solutions]] deposit box on Tau Sigma 7 tells crews who deliver samples to report to Hardlight Station for payment.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/the dyson initiative\|The Dyson Initiative]] seeks a salvage crew through Hardlight Station's Interstellar Opportunities board to recover a missing corporate freighter carrying classified hyperdrive technology. The only clues are scrambled signals from Achernar. The freighter is the CF Senespera, which disappeared one year ago.
- Prisoners who work on the moon Netjeru can come from Prison Station Echo.
