# Karth-Mani

No source names this region; the label is unofficial.

Karth is a tiny mesa-studded moon on the galaxy's far edge, on the edge of known space. When the blue gas giant Tabernas eclipses it, night lasts a week. Relic orbital satellites shoot down all ships or flying objects, so the only way down or up is an ancient space elevator. The first human colonists on Karth fought a war with the Wigoy. Mani is a tidally locked ocean world where people live in eternal sunset. Its colony was established as an aquaculture and food production facility for feeding the distant garden moon of Karth. S-4813 is an isolated rimworld buffeted by eternal windstorms.

## Locations

### Karth

- Space elevator top terminal: where all travelers to Karth arrive
- Larstown: the boomtown at the ancient elevator's base, named for Col. Lars Tanaka, who led the first MEF expedition. Its sites include the First Bank of Larstown, the Bronze Statue of Lars Tanaka, the Desperado Karaoke Bar, the Admiral Grace Inn and the General Store Emporium.
- [[workshop/mosh/modules/Arkady's Outpost\|Arkady's Outpost]]: an independent trading outpost on the western edge of Larstown
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/krth\|KRTH]], The Voice of Karth: a broadcast tower and studio west of Larstown, heard from the outskirts of Larstown to Krieg Ranch
- Krieg Ranch: an isolated sheet metal homestead beside a creaking windmill
- Silver Spire: the Dawnseekers' silver metal spire, snapped near the top and surrounded by Donor Huts
- Seahorse Mine: Larstown's first Wigoy chunk prospecting operation
- Ship Graveyard: the remnants of the Old People's fallen ships, covered by rolling dunes in a broad valley
- Lookout Tower: a Valley Rangers base whose beacon fire warns Fort Vee Cee of invaders
- Old Rejuvenation Center: a broken villa compound near Fort Vee Cee that the Valley Rangers avoid

### Mani

- Last Atoll: the only land on Mani and its only population center, a Class A port in the shadow of a crashed colony ship
- Spaceport Docks: where stevedores unload the latest catch of hagfish
- Wet Market: the main bazaar of the Last Atoll and the traditional center of the Mareist faith
- Ship Palace: a gutted twenty-story former generation ship, now the chrome palace of the Land Barons
- Sharpe Co. Compound: a closed enclave for corpos, tourists and employees on the former site of half of the Landed District
- Leeshade: the largest portion of the Last Atoll, where most of the population lives
- Thermidor Coffeehouse: a coffeehouse in the Landed District whose performance space is a hotbed of revolutionary sentiment
- Floatholm: a scrap metal floating settlement just offshore
- Folly Rock Prison: a prison on a gray stack of rocks that holds dissidents and criminals
- Sacred Grotto: the grotto that became the home of Revival Mareism
- Platform 11: the remnants of an ancient aquaculture platform, 20m below the surface
- Admiral Graze: an ancient cargo ship stolen from the Land Barons, now a floating settlement for those who refuse to bend the knee
- Aurora Diamond Facility: Sharpe Co.'s offshore aurora diamond mining operation, served by hovertrain
- Tinea Weather Station: a Sharpe Co. station, a gunmetal gray wheel 500m across, commissioned to study Mani's weather

### S-4813

- Sable: Outpost S-4813, a town on the fringes where modular aluminum shacks surround a few company buildings in the red rocks
- Kadaar Mining Facility: a rhodium mine with five decades of horrendous conditions, the site of a miners' uprising seven years ago
- Elmo's Canteen: a dark and smoky bar, Sable's beating heart since the town was founded
- Old Union Hall: a meeting space burned out by a past corporate sting

## Factions

- [[workshop/mosh/companies/sharpe co\|Sharpe Co.]]: an interstellar mining megacorp with full extraction rights to Mani's aurora diamond seam. It exports the diamonds to the sector's rich. It is the corporate ruler of Sable on S-4813, and its flagship is the John Henry.
- Kadaar Order: formed to fight for the liberation of the miners from corporate oppression, with the aim to liberate the sector from Sharpe Co.'s yoke
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/the black riders\|Black Riders]]: mercenaries from the frigid steppes of Caestus who wear blacked-out battle armor. Sharpe Co. hires them.
- Manian Expeditionary Force (MEF): colonial marines from Mani on guard and administration duty on Karth. They hold Larstown and charge visitors an entrance fee and an exit tax on Wigoy chunks.
- Dawnseekers: immortal rulers who reign over their Donor servants
- Valley Rangers: a cargo cult formed around the Lunar Park Service's traditions, devoted to protecting Karth's natural landscape and creatures
- Wigoy: extraterrans, Karth's original inhabitants
- Sandsquid cult: a feared sandsquid-worshipping cult in the farthest reaches of Karth's desert
- Red Mamba Posse: a gang of four outlaws led by Willis "Wild Hog" Hardy, a man wanted in seven systems
- Landed Nobles: the ancestral rulers of the Last Atoll. House Jörgessen holds the Land Baron's Throne and House Tanaka the position of High Priest.
- Tide Priests: preachers of Revival Mareism, exiled from the Last Atoll
- Holy Divers: mutant people with gills and slick, green skin
- Atoll Guilds: the guilds that control most labor on the Last Atoll
- Chromefolk: Starborn people with silver irises, exiled from their generation ship. Chromefolk Pirates from the Admiral Graze are seen as folk heroes.

## Connections

- The first MEF expedition, led by Col. Lars Tanaka, discovered Karth "for the good of Mani". House Tanaka navigators rediscovered the lost moon using a spaceship acquired from Sharpe Co., and a Manian offworld colony there began exporting the immortality drug Wigoy Powder.
- The cargo lander The Long Goodbye, from Karth, collects Wigoy Powder at Mani's Spaceport Docks.
- Governor Steig Tanaka of Larstown is the estranged daughter of Matron Hildegard Tanaka of House Tanaka on Mani.
- [[workshop/mosh/modules/Arkady's Outpost\|Arkady's Outpost]] on Karth sells Manian Sealia, a drug synthesized from an aquatic plant found in the darkest depths of the Manian oceans.
- KRTH's musical rotation is mainly Manian sea shanties and whatever cassettes Sharp has dug up while prospecting.
- The cargo ship Terus Maju has its home port on Karth and its destination at Prospero's Dream. Its compressed water is earmarked for the MEF on Karth.
- Ensign Richard Blacksburg, head of [[workshop/mosh/companies/gharstadt & tyr mining corporation\|G-T Corp]] operations on Netjeru, used to be a corporal for the MEF on Karth.
- The Essence Medspa & Wellness Center in the Xola Healing Dome has Karth sand baths and stocks self-heating Karth sand.
- A Bleached One, a remnant of a strange alien species from the rimspace moon of Karth, is among the creatures in the Bloodfields at Blackstar Station.
- Arkady's Outpost sells the latest issues of the zine [[workshop/mosh/modules/Analog Survey N.1\|Analog Survey]] and gives away Arkady's pamphlet *Never Trust a Tinkerer*, which is also among the trinkets of the [[workshop/mosh/sectors/Hecate Sector\|Hecate Sector]].
- Spirox Vane, the most senior Speaker of the Kadaar Order, has a track titled "Kadaar Ashram Monastery" on the *Many Moons* mixtape.
- Arkady Labrynthe belts out Prince Blaster tunes at the Desperado most nights. Prince Blaster's song "Second Step Beyond" is number 2 on the Hot Five chart in the [[workshop/mosh/sectors/Hecate Sector\|Hecate Sector]].
