# Combined Systems

Tremendously Low Frequency (TLF) transmissions can be received across the Combined Systems. Penalties for breaching site confidentiality at TEL 022 include blacklisting on the Combined Systems industry register.

## Locations

- UPB 154 (unadopted planetary body 154): an uninhabited planet in the Stroop Belt, classed as hostile for colonisation, with extremely saline water. Beamish Dust, runaway nanoparticles designed for proof-of-concept terraforming, is assumed to be present in its atmosphere.
- TEL 022, "Thousand Empty Light": a steel tunnel about one kilometre long and the only known artificial structure on UPB 154, maintained by [[workshop/mosh/companies/hazmos corp\|Hazmos Corp.]] under a service contract. A caisson descends to it from an offshore platform. It sits on a rock outcrop.

## Factions

- [[workshop/mosh/companies/hazmos corp\|Hazmos Corp.]]: describes itself as "the quiet constant keeping the lights on." Its force of lamplighters maintains the most challenging sites under its purview.
- Ham Ultra Mono society: a collective of enthusiasts keeping the subradiosport alive.

## Connections

- Moses Dross, President of the mineral conglomerate Siluvium Ore, appeals on a bounty line for information about his adult son, last seen on a transit vessel in the Stroop Belt. UPB 154 lies in the Stroop Belt.
- The ORACLE procedure has personnel make visual checks of their surroundings with reference to the Combined Systems Semiotic Standard. Maintenance at TEL 022 follows the ORACLE system.
