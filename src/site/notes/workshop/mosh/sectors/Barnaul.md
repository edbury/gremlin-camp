# Barnaul Jump-1 Cluster

The Barnaul Jump-1 Cluster is a small Jump-1 cluster out in the middle of rimspace. Its star systems are each connected by a Jump-1 hyperspace route, and a Jump-3 route runs from Waystation 61-B to the Banquo cluster.

## Systems

- Adoni: terrestrial dwarf planet with a toxic atmosphere and a turbulent climate. Ships can refuel there.
- Aleph Gate Higher Learning Facility: a remodeled J2C-I Executive Transport.
- Waystation 61-B: SEBACO autonomous factory zone and B-class port, near a massive Jump-5 derelict hulk.
- [[workshop/mosh/systems/Samsa System\|Samsa System]]: Samsa VI is a terrestrial, earth-sized planet with a terraformed atmosphere and a rainy climate, home to Greta Base, a C-class port. A skeleton crew established Greta Base and Heron Terraforming Station on Samsa VI.
- Ypsilon 14: a SEBACO asteroid mining station and C-class port, a remote asteroid mining base.
- Olkhova: Olkhova Moon Base, a colonial settlement and B-class port.
- Barnaul-7: liquid mini-giant planet with a hellish climate and a deep sea research base, a C-class port.

## Factions

- [[workshop/mosh/companies/sebaco\|SEBACO Mining Ltd.]]: interstellar asteroid mining and planetary colonization megacorporation, with locations at Ypsilon 14, Olkhova Moon Base, Barnaul-7 and Waystation 61-B.
- Aleph Gate church: present at Adoni.
- Interstellar Asteroid Miner's Union: present at Ypsilon 14 and Olkhova.

## Connections

- Sonya, team leader at the Ypsilon 14 base, is one of SEBACO Mining Ltd.'s VIPs.
- The cargo freighter Tarcza, a Jump-1 Type C freighter, is bound for Ypsilon 14.
- Lilith, a 32-year-old crewmember, has a wife on Ypsilon 14.
- The colony ship Discovery One was headed to Samsa VI.
- Graffiti at Waystation 61-B reads "DON'T FIGHT. FIGHTING ONLY PISSES IT OFF.".
