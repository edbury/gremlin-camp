# Hecate Sector

The Hecate Sector is a sparsely settled rim belt. It is hardly benign, and neither are its inhabitants. Rim farmers, asteroid miners, cryptozoologists and Void Musicians come from the sector. Local goods include Hecatean wine and Hecate Swill. A Ghost Dance Mixtape of very rare Hekatean political songs is worth 1k+ to the right buyers.

## Locations

- Hecate Sector Station: a two-level [[workshop/mosh/companies/pure transmission\|Pure Transmission]] orbital broadcasting station orbiting the ice moon Boreas, with a large antenna bearing a blue neon "PURE" sign
- Hecate Cassette Archive: an archive where technicians catalog cassette tapes and securely archive them

## Factions

- [[workshop/mosh/companies/pure transmission\|Pure Transmission]]: designed the Hecate Sector Station operational algorithm to create the most listened-to broadcast in the sector
- Artemis Corporation: a sponsor of the Hecate Sector Station broadcast
- Analog Survey: a radical anarchist group. The collective is dedicated to the liberation of the auditory landscape through dissemination, preservation and occasionally direct action. It publishes the zine [[workshop/mosh/modules/Analog Survey N.1\|Analog Survey]], a catalog of the rarest and most righteous mixtapes in the galaxy. The zine is among the sector's trinkets.
- The Void: a church of dubious origins dedicated to the discovery of alien life and communion with the blackness of space, with its headquarters on Echo Station. Peyter of the Void, a cultist for hire who wants to contact alien life, belonged to it.
- Neo-Reactionary Coalition: a shadowy group that infects corporate organizations with fascist beliefs
- Void Musicians: a mysterious musical heritage
- Cult of Hekate: keeps its own ritual book
- Dwarf Star gang
- Intergalactic Agricultural Union

## Connections

- Pure Transmission runs Hecate Sector Station, and Pure Transmission Protocol requires an archival technician to inspect each tape before it enters the Hecate Cassette Archive.
- Issue #1 of [[workshop/mosh/modules/Analog Survey N.1\|Analog Survey]] can turn up on Hecate Sector Station alongside *Rimwise Dispatch 0002* and [[workshop/mosh/modules/Erno Noise\|Erno Noise]] 11.
- [[workshop/mosh/modules/Arkady's Outpost\|Arkady's Outpost]] on Karth sells the latest issues of [[workshop/mosh/modules/Analog Survey N.1\|Analog Survey]] and gives away Arkady's pamphlet *Never Trust a Tinkerer*. The pamphlet is among the sector's trinkets.
- Spirox Vane, the most senior Speaker of the Kadaar Order on S-4813, has a track titled "Kadaar Ashram Monastery" on the *Many Moons* mixtape.
- Prince Blaster's song "Second Step Beyond" is number 2 on the Hot Five chart. On Karth, Arkady Labrynthe belts out Prince Blaster tunes at the Desperado most nights.
