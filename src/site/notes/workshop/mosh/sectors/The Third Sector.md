# The Third Sector

The Third Sector holds high-sec space, such as the corespace Dice System, and pirate-ridden rimspace, such as the Narcissus System. Corporations post jobs across the sector, and several pirate factions operate in it, held in a balance of power. A rumor in the sector advises hiring marines when crossing Narcissus.

## Systems

- Dice System: high-sec corespace, Jump 2 from the Narcissus System, Jump 3 from the α¬Ω System and Jump 5 from the Eridani System.
	- The Skaana: a planetary trader and "free trade oasis in the black heart of the Dice system", and a casino station.
	- The Dinoplex: "A seedy dinosaur theme park on an aging, ring-shaped station".
	- Enceladus Research Facility: a scientific research facility at the south pole of the ice moon Enceladus.
- Narcissus System: pirate-ridden rimspace, Jump 2 from the Dice System and Jump 3 from the Eridani System.
	- Alcor Station: a seedy Alcor rest station. It is one of more than a hundred ALCOR stations that serve "cheap fuel cores, lousy “entertainment” and questionable culinary choices to all manners of spaceship crews".
	- Nergal Station: a station occupied by squatters.
	- The Lima: a derelict science vessel.
- Eridani System: Jump 5 from the Dice System and Jump 3 from the Narcissus System. A rumor holds that ambitious mining operations there went bust.
	- Muraldal: a barren ice planet, also spelled Murandal, and a terraforming candidate.
		- Outpost Choi: a research outpost on Muraldal that studies the habitats at the bottom of its oceans. It comprises the orbital station DeLange, the surface habitation Japline's Ache and Choi Labs on the ocean floor.
	- Shen Kuo Research Facility: a Takahashi Advanced Genetics facility on Ur, a jungle moon and the first moon of the jovian planet 51 Eridani B.
- α¬Ω System: Jump 1 from The Last Nebula and Jump 3 from the Dice System.
	- Azure Base: an "Astronomical research outpost on a small moon".
- The Last Nebula: Jump 1 from the α¬Ω System.
- Wandering anomalies:
	- The Black Pyramid: "an ancient pyramid floating in space".
	- The Graveyard: "a state-of-the-art, deep space survey vessel designed for a skeleton crew" that exits jump space in Sector SQ79.

## Factions

- [[workshop/mosh/companies/alcor fuel & services\|ALCOR Fuel & Services]]: a subsidiary of Shikewara-Tarmec Corp. and the company behind the ALCOR stations.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/azure horizons\|Azure Horizons Corporation]]: hires crews for a month-long security post and to find The Graveyard and retrieve its scan data.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/drex corporation\|Drex Corporation]]: hires crews to investigate missing ships near an unidentified superstructure and to eliminate the squatters on Nergal Station. It sends a representative as a consultant on an expedition to the Black Pyramid.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/dyson phytology\|Dyson Phytology]]: established operations on Muraldal and hires crews to retrieve Outpost Choi research data and a sample.
- E.R.F. Project: hires crews for the monthly Enceladus Research Facility resupply.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/pantech pharmaceutical\|Pantech Pharmaceutical]]: hires crews to salvage the derelict science vessel Lima. Murmurs of a Pantech scouting party discovering flora with regenerative properties circulate throughout the sector.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/syngen\|SynGen]]: brokers a bounty on Ur fauna samples. The Dinoplex's hangar blast doors require SynGen access.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/takahashi advanced genetics\|Takahashi Advanced Genetics]]: a corporation dedicated to research into expanding the human genome, which sent a research expedition to the Eridani system.
- Pirates: they hire crews to hunt snitches hiding in unexplored space and to heist Alcor Station fuel cores.
- Private collectors: they pay for genetic samples from the Dinoplex.

## Connections

- Dyson Phytology is a competitor of [[workshop/mosh/companies/perigee corporation\|Perigee Corporation]] and pays premium bounties for samples of Meridian's ice-VII layer and for recordings of its ocean bed activity.
- Pantech Pharmaceutical and Takahashi Advanced Genetics are among the companies that hire crews to investigate reports of an alien structure suddenly appearing, at 10kcr per chamber explored.
- Koji Smith lost his job as an entertainer at an ALCOR station to animatronics.
