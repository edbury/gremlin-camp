# Carbex

No source names this region; the label is unofficial.

The Dream, a sector-wide broadcast event, takes place in the city of Salnati on Dalmorus VIII.

## Locations

- Carbex: mining world smaller than Earth, with an unbreathable atmosphere. Its only settlements support the mining operations excavating and exporting charcoal.
- Angelus Fabrica Station: refinery station in Carbex's low orbit that makes activated carbon.
- Sineto Deep Space Factory: Sineto Chem Solutions' factory station, about 24 hours of hyperspace travel from Carbex. Poker is the favored pastime of its residential district, the Sineto Spike, and the station hosts the annual Sector Tournament.
- Dalmorus VIII: planet with a population of approximately 2,121,000 that mainly exports salt. Its soil is too salty for agriculture, so food and biostuffs are its major imports.
- Thunziter system: Thunziter 2 and 3 hold permanent mining colonies on massive mineral and gem deposits.
- Thunziter 5: planet with unbreathable air whose oceans teem with wildlife, including giant sonic whales. Its colonies sit on domed derricks in the ocean, and its capital, Nerrivick, holds the planet's main spaceport. Hoss, who rose to power as leader of the local steelworkers union, is its governor.

## Factions

- [[workshop/mosh/companies/sineto chem solutions\|Sineto Chem Solutions]]: a major chemical company in the sector.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/starpoly strategic\|Starpoly Strategic]]: sells personal protection equipment to citizens across the sector.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/jexiflerbo capital\|JEXIFLERBO Capital]]: a family-oriented enterprise with 10 partners, dedicated to growing businesses in the sector.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/the toil and trouble collective\|The Toil & Trouble Collective]]: negotiates fair pay and working conditions on behalf of the working class throughout the galaxy.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/dextro-singleton spacial solutions\|Dextro-Singleton Spacial Solutions]]: transport and transit corporation specializing in experimental faster-than-light travel outside of traditional hyperspace technology.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/cornelia acquisitions & sales amalgam\|Cornelia Acquisitions & Sales Amalgam]] (CASA): holds a majority stake in most industries across the galaxy. Its founder, Cornelia, started The Dream 16 years ago.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/sovereign bliss inc\|Sovereign Bliss Inc.]]: luxury goods corporation and a power player in the sector. It bought exclusive industrial rights in the Thunziter system about 325 years ago, and its satellite office in Nerrivick stands abandoned.

## Connections

- Shipping manifests at the Angelus Fabrica list Sineto Chem Solutions' chemicals, and Sineto owns nearly all the salt mines on Dalmorus VIII.
- The medical research vessel Chiron has its home port at the Sineto Deep Space Factory.
