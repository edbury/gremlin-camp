# Eden Prime

No source names this region; the label is unofficial.

Eden Prime is a long-lost colony founded by the Seekers of Eden, who reached it on a generation ship before the advent of slipspace.

## Locations

- Eden Prime: a planet covered in lush jungles, verdant fields and teeming oceans, settled with the aid of the ship's AI, Mother.
- Sonata Survey Colony (Moonbase Sonata): a colony in a large crater on Carpathia II, the moon of the gas giant Carpathia I. It went dark 150 years ago while collecting geological data.
- Jericho 475: a mineral-rich frontier planetoid of fertile mesas and deep ravines. Darkstone Mining Solutions runs most of its mining colonies, and some are wildcat operations.
- The Prophet's Mesa: a Triumvirate mission colony on a mesa 15 km across on Jericho 475. Most visitors arrive by a shuttle that runs to orbit and other colonies.

## Factions

- Church of the Holy Triumvirate: a humanist evolution of Christianity whose members hold that the soul resides in the brain, heart and liver.
- Seekers of Eden: a zealous sect of the Church of the Holy Triumvirate with a vision of recreating Eden.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/darkstone\|Darkstone]]: Darkstone Conglomerates sold the Seekers their AI, Mother. A researcher at Darkstone For-Profit Galactic University (DFPGU) has discovered Eden Prime's coordinates.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/holmwood mining solutions\|Holmwood Mining Solutions]]: wants a return on its investment in the Sonata Survey Colony.

## Connections

- The Prophet of the Triumvirate mission colony on Jericho 475 speaks of a universe without death or sickness, a new Eden. After the Jericho 475 incident, the Church of the Holy Triumvirate hires a crew to escort a cardinal to Eden Prime, the Prophet's home planet.
- A Darkstone research pod studies the Radiant Nexus, an anomalous point of light on the far rim of known space, with the permission of the monks who live there.
- Dark Stone Security is stationed at the Xola Healing Dome, a corporate retreat on the (216) Kleopatra asteroid colony. The Dark Stone corporate mascot is "Stoney".
- Dr. E. P. Calcium of the Dark Stone Theological Exploitation Dept. lectures on "Black Hole" cults at the 5th Quinquennial Interstellar Interdisciplinary Conference.
- Dark Stone Disposal Services is the contact when Earthly Flesh pseudo flesh begins speaking.
