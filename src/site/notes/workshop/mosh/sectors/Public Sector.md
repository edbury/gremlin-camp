# Public Sector

The Public Sector is a sector of space dominated by the Publico megacorporation. The megacorporation reaches into every facet of Public Sector life, extracting labor and smothering leisure. Its subsidiaries jockey for position in an eternal power cycle, and few organizations survive outside the Company's umbrella. Common travel options include GreenStar shuttles, jump liners and Upstairs Corp yachts.

## Systems

- Love System, in corespace: Lord Wesley's Rise, the Hodder Forest Reserve Habitat and The Silver Noose
- Joy System, in rimspace: Fairpoint Rock, Smythe's Planet, Siesta-3 and Mandella IV

- Hope System, in frontier space: Hope Hyperlane Construction, Hope V and the Seref Generation Ship
- Lord Wesley's Rise: a ring-shaped S-Class corespace station (callsign LC-32), an idyllic daydream-land for corporate management.
- Fairpoint Rock: a dead planet.
- Siesta-3: on most starmaps the station pings as Automated Station W-900CJ-45. It shares the cut corners of GreenStar Logistics' rest stations, but GreenStar freighters no longer stop there. Those in the know call it the Siesta-3 Autonomous Zone, an anarchist commune of pirates, artists, hackers and teamsters.
- Persei 19: a small mining station on a moon, housing about 3000 people. Most of its technology was obsolete decades ago, mining operations continue, and cash-rich miners with nowhere to go fuel a healthy underground economy. Adam, a member of the Public Teamsters Union, was murdered there.
- Facility Delta: a remote [[workshop/mosh/companies/hylix-ventross systems\|Hylix-Ventross]] corporate research facility, site of an ongoing Class-3 xenobiological exposure incident.

## Factions

- [[workshop/mosh/companies/publico\|Publico]]: corporate oversight and contingency planning
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/upstairs corp\|Upstairs Corp]]: station construction, luxury products and services
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/university of idalia minor\|University of Idalia Minor]]: exploration, planetology and xenobiology research
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/arbitration limited\|Arbitration Limited]]: Publico's correctional subsidiary, handling contract enforcement, corrections and debt farming
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/hylix-ventross systems\|Hylix-Ventross Systems]]: communications, technology and robotics development
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/blackshield\|Blackshield Mercenary Group]]: military contractors and private security
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/greenstar logistics\|GreenStar Logistics]]: shipbuilding, hyperspace tech, freight and freight infrastructure
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/abattoir family co\|Abattoir Family Co.]]: office supply and IP retention products, and espionage
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/james hardly's adventure galactic\|James Hardly's Adventure Galactic]]: terraforming, colonization and survival products
- Public Teamsters Union: labor surveillance and workforce coordination
- Mining Workers' Union: pays for the investigation into Adam's murder as part of his insurance package
- Westies Crime Syndicate: black market exploitation and underground corporate dealings
- Rimspace Liberation Front: rimspace liberation evangelism and radical terrorism
- Omegism: an illegal android singularity cult with a cross-species unity philosophy
- Interstellar Mega Mart (ISMM): listed by the Company as an unknown entity

- The inhabitants of Siesta-3 took out a loan during a desperate lean period.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/hylix-ventross systems\|Hylix-Ventross Labs]] designs radiation-shielded arcologies for miners and researchers on the moon Netjeru.
