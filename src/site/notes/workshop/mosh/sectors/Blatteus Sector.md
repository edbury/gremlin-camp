# Blatteus Sector

Interstellar corporations control the Blatteus Sector. The United Terran Alliance (UTA), a hostile foreign government, operates beyond it.

## Locations

- Calliope System: home to many sensitive R&D projects for the interstellar corporations that control the sector. Its remote tropical moon, Agamemnon, holds the Valley of Dreams.
- NuMiami: tropical moon controlled by the Protectorate. Its city, Laser Cove, is billed as a galactic leisure and business destination.
- Seashell Luxury Retirement Condo Community: retirement community in Laser Cove serving some of the most powerful retired persons in the sector.
- The Devil's Triangle: a section of the Blatteus Sector holding a red nebula cluster.

## Factions

- New Dawn Pharmatek: one of the original interstellar megacorporations based in the Blatteus Sector.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/the protectorate\|The Protectorate]]: a private military peacekeeping force in the employ of corporate paymasters.
- The Kurzi Kutlar, "the Red Wolves": a dangerous Blatteus criminal syndicate.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/indo-dynamic\|Indo-Dynamic]]: corporation whose One Indo-Dynamic Tower in Laser Cove is a laser research and development facility and marketing headquarters.

## Connections

- The Protectorate controls NuMiami, and a Protectorate patrol vessel enters the Calliope System every 7 solar days.
- The Seashell Luxury Retirement Condo Community in Laser Cove serves several former UTA dissident political leaders.
- A crashed New Anglican Blood Pirate scout vessel lies in the jungle of the Valley of Dreams. Blood Pirates are criminals known for attacking high occupancy starships in deep space.
- Kyle Markus, codename "2-Ways", a Black Krokodil inmate, snapped after his brother disappeared in the Merciful Dawn deep space transport disaster. The CTS Merciful Dawn is a deep space commercial transport ship.
