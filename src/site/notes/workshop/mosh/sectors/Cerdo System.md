# Cerdo System

The Cerdo System holds the colony world Cerdo's Garden and the space station TAMA-81.

## Locations

- Cerdo's Garden: a planetary colony under the Forever Rain. Heartroot, a meaty potato-beet high in protein, is a staple there.
- Marcion's Abbey: a half-ruined abbey on Cerdo's Garden.
- Slate River: a river on Cerdo's Garden that leads to the Oceanic shores. Its tributaries and natural dams shift weekly, so maps are less reliable than a good guide.
- TAMA-81: a space station with farms in its Bernal Sphere and a mining base downwell. Ballistic weapons are taboo and illegal on board.
- TAMA-81 Corporate Ring: holds the Electorenzo Resources reception and R&D labs, a VIP Company Affiliate Clinic, a Tempura Veritas arms vendor office, Shintopraxis Park, the 49th Apollonians' mercenary quarters, the [[workshop/mosh/companies/proxi mt\|Proxi M.T.]] Android Repair Workshop, the Order's St. Iria's Chapel & Monastery, the [[workshop/mosh/companies/gyrogate agency\|Gyrogate Agency]] and Freezer Operations cryostorage.

## Factions

- The Order of the Apostle's Gate: followers of the Five Tenets spread by Cerdo the Traveler, led by a High Sister. It is the de facto police force of TAMA-81.
- Defenders of God's Gate: a monastic order of the Order of the Apostle's Gate that keeps Marcion's Abbey.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/electorenzo resources\|Electorenzo Resources]] (E/R): the Company on TAMA-81, an extreme minority living in manors and gated communities in the Company Estates.
- Major Miner's Union (MMU): the majority of TAMA-81's inhabitants within the Bernal Sphere.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/sons of apollo\|Sons of Apollo]]: mercenaries hired to protect Company assets on TAMA-81.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/tempura veritas united\|Tempura Veritas United]]: creator of the Plated Hog Hounds, a genemod species found on TAMA-81.

## Connections

- Clampers ruin crops on Cerdo's Garden and infest TAMA-81.
- Ori Moineaux, an Electorenzo Resources executive, is wanted on TAMA-81 for the illegal retention of Union records. The Order and the Miner's Union have put up a bounty for him.
- A report from a makeshift mining base describes Rainblades that "drink in the Forever Rain", the rain that falls on Cerdo's Garden.
- Simone Vett, CEO of [[workshop/mosh/companies/electorenzo resources\|Electorenzo Resources]], gives the keynote at the 5th Quinquennial Interstellar Interdisciplinary Conference at [[workshop/mosh/companies/kiabi university\|Kiabi University]], which Electorenzo Resources and Tempura Veritas United sponsor.
- Electorenzo Resources lays claim to the backwater Poe system, where the E/R Poe-V Aerostat Station floats in the atmosphere of the gas giant Poe-V.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/tempura veritas united\|Tempura Veritas United]] is the arms vendor and a corporate sponsor of [[workshop/mosh/companies/cendrillon mercenaries\|Cendrillon Mercenaries]].
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/cendrillon mercenaries\|Cendrillon Mercenaries]] assumed Ori Moineaux did not want to pay for mercenaries because the Company provided them.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/proxi mt\|Proxi M.T.]], which has an Android Repair Workshop on TAMA-81, sponsors the Con/Sen android factory and calls itself a Rimspace Merchant Company Affiliate working throughout the Rim. It supplied Android workshop assistants to the 5th Quinquennial Interstellar Interdisciplinary Conference. Its Human Science Department issues the Sentience Assessment Procedure.
- The Android Rights Association calls Proxi M.T. "a Coreworld Company who sell Androids as slaves" and protests outside the Con/Sen storefront. Proxi M.T. was the first company to negotiate with the ARA.
- Jerryvine berries grow in the gardens of Marcion's Abbey, and the jerryvine comes from Mwana II. Mwana II's jerryvines are studied for their role in the disappearance of the Ukisi, a xeno civilization.
