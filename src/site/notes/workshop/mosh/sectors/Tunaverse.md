# Tunaverse

Smugglers and vendors work in the Tunaverse, and for anyone who needs anonymity or lacks the time to procure wares the legal way, "there is always a vagabond or two with incredible means to obtain what you want". Its known places range from the agricultural Julip IV System to dangerous, nearly uninhabitable planets such as Celsio-X and Trace Atko.

## Locations

- Julip IV System: the planet Julip IV and its satellites Atlas Roe, Gilgamesh Red and Uruk 1, linked by a shuttle system that runs three trips a day. Travel to Uruk 1 is invite only.
	- Julip IV: a planet with a B-class port, settled 643 years ago by inhabitants of the Zadari Solar System. It depends on its massive agricultural industry, and most of its produce is powderized, sealed and processed into nutrient-dense meal supplements for export. All land on the planet is owned by the Julipian Government. Its satellites and surrounding planets depend heavily on its produce, and it has no substantial defense of its own. "BUY JULIP!" ads promote it as a planet "dedicated to agriculture".
	- Atlas Roe: the only natural satellite of Julip IV, with a C-class port. All of its land is privately owned by a few hundred individuals.
	- Gilgamesh Red: a station with an A-class port and Julip IV's commercial satellite, now a prominent visiting site. Each business owner on the station holds a stake in it.
	- Uruk 1: a station with an S-class port that houses the mixed government responsible for all of Julip IV's satellites, which splits powers between the Solano royal family, the aristocracy and democratically elected politicians. It is the smallest and most protected satellite of Julip IV.
- The Ship Canterbury: "The supposed space station designed as a cathedral floats somewhere in rim space. It’s said to be a treasure trove of ancient xenotech."
- The Merlin Dome: the main barracks and training grounds of the Knights of Lazarus, just outside the metropolitan city Grand Marseilles. Tours are given to the visiting public on Thursdays and Fridays.
- Celsio-X: a blizzarding wasteland with thousands of massive burrows, where Chisel & Mallet runs mining operations. Lidar maps of its burrows rate their nest likelihood and survivability.
- Trace Atko: an arid wasteland close to its sun that was once a well-established titanium-mining colony.
- Venus 3: with Celsio-X and Trace Atko, one of the nearly uninhabitable lands "known to be dangerous, too expensive to settle, and near impossible to maintain".
- Jiri Clovis Bunker: a bunker on a privately owned dust world armed with mobile anti-air cannons and human-driven drones.
- The junk moon: an ever-growing planetoid of junk whose Core "controls the jumps that send the moon across the sectors". The Executive Suits, the Reclaimers and the Bloody Burrowers live there.

## Factions

- [[workshop/mosh/modules/Knights of Lazarus\|Knights of Lazarus]]: an order "renowned for being charitable contributors and purveyors of ancient knowledge". Publicly, people know who they are and see them as charitable. The Steel Order is "the only public-facing order in the KoL" and offers protective services and mediation in interplanetary conflicts. Steel Order Infantry 698 is one of its units. The Knights Clarion and the Pelican Crested are the order's other sub-orders.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/luko fin corp\|Luko Fin Corp]]: a mission-outsource agency.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/yucatan tech co (company)\|Yucatan Tech Co]]: a weapons maker with the slogan "Unique weapons For Unique circumstances". Adonna Camsal is its Chief Weapons Engineer.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/laughing sphinx corporation\|Laughing Sphinx Corporation]]: a space debris cleanup company whose cleaning units include Space Debris Removal Crew XIX.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/united fleet delivery\|United Fleet Delivery]]: a shipping corporation that delivers construction and repair spacecraft, debris collectors and everything needed to maintain space life.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/hidden persons inc\|Hidden Persons Inc]]: a protective services agency that gives displaced people hunted by bad actors a new and safe home.
- [[workshop/mosh/companies/blackfog defense services\|Blackfog Defense Services]]: a mercenary company.
- Eudaimonia: a psychological tech corporation.
- Knob Adjustment: a covert group that provides revolutionaries with "blueprints, floor plans, records, leads, EMPs, weapons, and direction".
- Iron Tiger: "The most notorious vigilante hacker group in the sector", with "a long history in doxxing bad actors and aiding rebellions".
- Imaginaria: a bioterrorist group and space horror dealer.
- Chainmail Street Gang: a street gang on Atlas Roe.
- Grey Terror Cartel: a former pirate syndicate best known for gunrunning specialized weaponry, taken down six years ago by the hacker group The Spark.

## Connections

- Santos Saul, a former president of Julip IV, is a frontrunner to lead the station Yumera Delta 3 after the Knights of Lazarus helped overthrow the dictatorial leader Anna Scurry.
- Vicente Cro leads the government-funded Agricultural Robotics Trust on Julip IV. The prototype compactor bot C-Bot H-34, named Humberto, was lost in transit when Vicente sent it to Zoya Amari, and it now travels the junk moon, compacting junk.
- Sichelgaita, a former Steel Order Seneschal of the Knights of Lazarus, crash landed on the junk moon and is now the Reclaimers' lead recon agent.
- The mercenary brothers Giannis and Dylly Macky left the Julip IV system after Giannis worked with the Chainmail Street Gang on Atlas Roe and Dylly worked for the transgenic silk-producing sheep farmer Gan Bataa on Julip IV.
- Rebels can buy records, blueprint data and holographic planetary maps from Chroniclers, FloorPlan Locators and Topographics, businesses on Gilgamesh Red, and can also turn to Hidden Persons Inc and Iron Tiger.
- Davin says he took the map book *Maps From Outside My Galaxy* from the Ship Canterbury, and the book *Cloning the Black Prince* is "Another Davin find from the Ship Canterbury".
- Stims from the dark web are developed from DNA and Space Horror samples extracted from Celsio-X and Trace Atko.
