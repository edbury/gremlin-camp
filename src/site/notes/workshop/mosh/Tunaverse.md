# Tunaverse

Spicy Tuna's shared setting, centered on the Julip IV system.

## Locations

- Julip IV System: Julip IV (B-class planet), Atlas Roe (C-class natural satellite), Gilgamesh Red (A-class station), Uruk 1 (S-class station), Gan Bataa
- Zadari Solar System
- The Ship Canterbury: home of the Knights of Lazarus, "somewhere in rim space"
- Jiri Clovis Bunker
- The Junky Moon of [[workshop/mosh/modules/Orbital Debris\|Orbital Debris]]: drifts between sectors; [[workshop/mosh/modules/Spicy Tie-Ins\|Spicy Tie-Ins]] brings it into the Tunaverse

## Factions

- Knights of Lazarus
- Yucatan Tech Co
- Luko Fin Corp
- Laughing Sphinx Corporation
- Celsio-X
- Steel Order Infantry 698
