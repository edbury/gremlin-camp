# Helios Inc

Helios Inc is an energy giant that chartered the Cormeia-9 mining colony to mine ship fuel.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/The Earth Above\|The Earth Above]]: holder of the Cormeia-9 mining colony charter, which wants its fuel mine running again.

## Known Facilities

- Cormeia-9 (Cor-9) mining colony: colony with one of the only working mines for unrefined ship fuel in the sector, overrun by the Pestilence
