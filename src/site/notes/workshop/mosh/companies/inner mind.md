# Inner Mind

Inner Mind is a corp whose entry in The Fat 10 praises it as the most pleasant employer in language that breaks down into repeated "yes". At the 5th Quinquennial Interstellar Interdisciplinary Conference, an Inner Mind Research Division presents work on the connection between prostheses and the Id; the text doesn't say whether this is the same company.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/The Fat 10\|The Fat 10]]: one of the ten top corps in Desdemona's Reach profiled in the guide.
