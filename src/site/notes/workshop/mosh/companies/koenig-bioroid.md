# Koenig-Bioroid

Koenig-Bioroid is a pharmaceutical megacorp specialising in clones and organic androids, with a subsidiary named 5TASTIC CEREALS.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Mechs in the City\|Mechs in the City]]: pharmaceutical megacorp with its own telepath faction entry, sole patent-holder of the telepathy drug.

## Known Products

- Telepathy drug: pill for awakening telepathy, manufactured only by Koenig-Bioroid
- Koenig-Bioroid Entertainment Console 5: banned game system that inflicted some players with irremovable telepathy
- Tastebud-adapting cereals: cereal line
- Clone workers: clones for manual labour on colony worlds
