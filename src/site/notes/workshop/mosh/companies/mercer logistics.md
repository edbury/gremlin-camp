# Mercer Logistics

Mercer Logistics is a corporation founded and run by its CEO Alphonse Mercer, whose staff crew deep space transport vessels.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Rane in Blood\|Rane in Blood]]: the transport corporation whose crew, key cards and proprietary androids run the Merciful Dawn.

## Known Products

- Commercial Service Androids: androids proprietary to Mercer transport vessels, built to service ship's systems and monitor sleeping crew
- Mercer Key Cards: Red and Yellow key cards that open restricted areas of the Merciful Dawn

## Known Vessels

- CTS Merciful Dawn: Corrino Sojourner-class deep space commercial transport with cryopods for 260 passengers, near the end of her service cycle

## Known Facilities

- Mercer Logistics corporate office (location of the Warden's choice): alternative starting point for the adventure
