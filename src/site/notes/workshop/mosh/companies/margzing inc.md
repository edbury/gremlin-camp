# Margzing Inc.

Margzing Inc. is a manufacturer of biological weapons and genetically engineered gear sold to private security, mercenaries and heavy industry.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Alien Armory\|Alien Armory]]: bio-weapon maker behind several catalogue items, with a black-market reseller NPC.

## Known Products

- DD-02 "Bonecrusher": experimental multi-use biological weapon with Fist and Rifle configurations
- LX77-05: sniper rifle using special 12.7 rounds made of brass and cartilage
- KG Muscle Shirt: genetically engineered biojacket that augments the wearer's strength
- Markson's Feet: organic footwear whose patent the Confederation reportedly sold to Margzing, due on the market the next year
