# Cordelv Mining

Cordelv Mining is a mining company that has been tapping the gas reserves of the planetoid W-37 for 24 years.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Abyssal\|Abyssal]]: the gas-mining company behind Rig 17, where the crew are sent to rescue survivors after a disaster.
