# Tempest Mercenary Company

Tempest Mercenary Company is a detachment of the Czernobog Private Military Corporation whose core mission is to act as muscle for the Golyanovo II Bratva.

## Known Facilities

- Tempest Company HQ (Prospero's Dream): headquarters with habitat, operations, armament, research and containment levels and the commander's mansion
- Tempest Co. Black Site: site holding a captive in a Hull Breach investigation
