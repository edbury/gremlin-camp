# Cruz

Cruz is a sports brand selling prosthetics, sportswear and drinks through its many retail locations, and has moved into weapons technology.

## Known Products

- ViViFi Soda: Cruz-branded collab soda with PacyGen Pharmaceuticals and Soft Drinks Company
- Cruz 4Ns: trendy sports model lower-leg prosthetics; new model P-4Ns on preorder
- SA-TR: full leg prosthetic system for elite athletes, +10 Speed
- CN-TR: quadrupedal waist-down mechanisation, +10 Speed and +5 Strength
- Cruz G-Suit: iconic suit, 1AP
- Cruz Padded Gym Gear: moisture-wicking padded gear, 2AP
- Cruz Buzzball Armor: armor for the bloodsport buzzball, 3AP
- Cruz Pilot Visor: eye protection in six colours
- Cruz Folding Fan: limited rainbow edition fan
- Cruz Patch: Cruz logo patch

## Known Vessels

- Cruz flagship: branded ship stocked with Cruz products
