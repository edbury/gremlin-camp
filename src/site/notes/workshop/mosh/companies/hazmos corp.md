# Hazmos Corp.

Hazmos is a company whose force of lamplighters restores power and light at the challenging sites under its purview.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Thousand Empty Light\|Thousand Empty Light]]: the employer whose lamplighters, the solo player among them, restore power to TEL 022.

## Known Facilities

- TEL 022, "Thousand Empty Light" (UPB 154): site serviced by Hazmos under a service contract now in its fifth year, a five-section tunnel reached by a pneumatic caisson
