# Chunky Cheese's Eateria

Chunky Cheese's Eateria is a family-oriented restaurant and arcade franchise run by Chunky Inc, with an animal-themed house band, the Magnificent Chunky Animal Band, and a Lazer Tag arena.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Chunky Cheese's Eateria\|Chunky Cheese's Eateria]]: the restaurant franchise that is the pamphlet's subject and location.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/The House Always Wins\|The House Always Wins]]: appendix sponsor of The Dream, with its own sponsor entry and room rebrands.

## Known Products

- Lazer Tag: neon strobing free-for-all using repurposed combat androids that may malfunction and try to kill participants
- Thwack-A-Marmot: token-operated arcade game
- Chunky Cheese's Fantastic Foodery Menu: pizza, entrees, desserts and drinks

## Known Facilities

- Chunky Cheese's Eateria (over 63 locations across the galaxy): family dining, arcade and Lazer Tag venue where the Magnificent Chunky Animal Band plays every 10 minutes
