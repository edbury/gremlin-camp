# Cendrillon Mercenaries

Cendrillon Mercenaries (also called the Cendrillon Mercenary Corps) is a Rimspace mercenary outfit that contracts out its mercenaries to clients.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Cendrillon Mercs for Hire\|Cendrillon Mercs for Hire]]: the product is the company's in-world brochure of mercenaries for hire.
