# Nakatomi Solutions

Nakatomi Solutions Inc. is a multi-armed megacorp, run by CEO Tomiichi "Tom" Yoshinobu, that gives its name to Nakatomi Corporate Sector 1365.

## Known Products

- Automated deposit box (ADB): person-sized metal box dropped from orbit to collect mineral samples
- PMAD (Polyhedral Memory Assistant Device): memory assistance device for completing paranormal event reports
- Unnamed capsule: captures and replays the last thoughts of recently deceased beings nearby; developed at the Nakatomi mortuary complex

## Known Facilities

- Hardlight Station (edge of the Epsilon Eridani system): commercial outpost run by Administrator Takako McClane of Nakatomi Solutions Inc.
- Tau Sigma 7 Nakatomi Orbital Station (Tau Sigma 7): former terraforming prospect, work halted when alleged alien ruins were found
- Nakatomi outpost (Tau Sigma 7): outpost securing minerals from a dig site
- Nakatomi mortuary complex (Medusa Station): mortuary complex where the capsule above was developed
- Nakatomi experimental settlement (Medusa 4): settlement near the caves of the Cave Siren
- Nakatomi Mental Wellness facility: site of NCID depositions
- Nakatomi Deprogramming Centre: mandatory wellness sessions and memory treatment after paranormal events
