# Nakatomi Solutions

Nakatomi Solutions Inc. is a multi-armed megacorp led by CEO Tomiichi "Tom" Yoshinobu, founded in Japan as an early private contractor in the race to colonize local space. It is a behemoth in the finance and interstellar construction industries. Nakatomi Corporate Sector 1365 bears its name.

## Subsidiaries and Other Names

- Nakatomi Security Contractors: security contractor on Hardlight Station

## Known Facilities

- Hardlight Station: commercial outpost at the edge of the Epsilon Eridani system
- Tau Sigma 7 Nakatomi Orbital Station: orbital station at Tau Sigma 7
- Nakatomi outpost: mineral outpost on Tau Sigma 7
- Nakatomi mortuary complex: mortuary complex on Medusa Station
- Nakatomi experimental settlement: experimental settlement on Medusa 4
- Nakatomi Mental Wellness facility: mental wellness facility
- Nakatomi Deprogramming Centre: corporate wellness centre
