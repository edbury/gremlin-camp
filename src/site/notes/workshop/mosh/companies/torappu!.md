# Torappu!

Torappu! is a manufacturer of enforcement and bounty hunting equipment and supplies.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Known Criminals of Orion\|Known Criminals of Orion]]: the pamphlet's sponsor, with a section of discounted bounty hunting gear.

## Known Products

- Scorpion Netgun: netgun with a sleek black design and an easy-to-use net cartridge system
- Aegis Maglock Handcuffs: titanium-alloy cuffs with an electromagnetic lock
- Hornet 1C Dart Rifle: non-lethal collapsible dart rifle with an 8-capacity magazine
- A1 Tracking Darts: ammo that attaches to most surfaces and broadcasts over 2500 km
- Hazard 6 Stun Darts: ammo that leaves most targets stunned, doubly effective on cyborgs and androids
