# TAXICO Foods

TAXICO Foods is a food corporation famous for its signature TAXICO hot sauces. It owns most of the island of Port TAXICO, which carries its name.

## Known Products

- TAXICO hot sauces: the corporation's signature hot sauces

## Known Facilities

- TAXICO greenhouses: hydroponic pepper greenhouses on Port TAXICO
