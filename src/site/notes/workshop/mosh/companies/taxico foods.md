# TAXICO Foods

TAXICO Foods is a food corporation famous for its signature TAXICO hot sauces that bought most of the island of Port TAXICO, including the name.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Hot Stuff on Shore Leave\|Hot Stuff on Shore Leave]]: owns most of the vacation island Port TAXICO and is one of its listed factions.

## Known Products

- TAXICO hot sauces: the corporation's signature hot sauces
- Safety compliance implants: implants TAXICO requires of its greenhouse workers

## Known Facilities

- Greenhouses (Port TAXICO): mammoth greenhouses where trapped workers produce exotic peppers
