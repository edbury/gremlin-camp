# Akina Aerial Systems

Akina Aerial Systems (AAS) is a supplier of aftermarket hover car modifications and equipment that hosts the Rigel V Classic antigravity race.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/The House Always Wins\|The House Always Wins]]: appendix sponsor of The Dream that scouts for AAS test pilots.

## Known Products

- AAS RC Hover Car: proportionally sized electromagnetic RC hover car
- AAS-Branded Compression Suit: 2 AP suit that aids Body Saves and Piloting
