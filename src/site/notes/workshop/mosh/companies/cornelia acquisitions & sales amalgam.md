# Cornelia Acquisitions & Sales Amalgam

Cornelia Acquisitions & Sales Amalgam (CASA) is the only galaxy-wide corporation, with a majority stake in most industries across the galaxy.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/The House Always Wins\|The House Always Wins]]: the corporation behind The Dream, whose CEO Cornelia set it up and which grants its own wish each year to the sponsor corporation that fulfills the most wishes.
