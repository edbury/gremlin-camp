# Faxxon Corp

Faxxon Corp is a large processing corporation discovered to be a cover for a black market drug manufacturer.

Role in *Knuckles Co*: target of the Faxxon Lab Heist one-shot and the client hiring Knuckles Co against its workers' union.

## Known Facilities

- Faxxon Processing Office B: houses the underground drug lab FXN LAB 04, which suffers an explosion
- Faxxon Processing Office H: houses the lower level drug lab FXN LAB 08, the heist location

## Appears in

- *Knuckles Co*
