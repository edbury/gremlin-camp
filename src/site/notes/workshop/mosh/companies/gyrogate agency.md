# Gyrogate Agency

Gyrogate Agency is an employer on the TAMA-81 station's Corporate Ring whose evac teams, androids and mercenaries operate there during the Clamper infestation.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Burn the Vermin on Tama-81\|Burn the Vermin on Tama-81]]: one of the two employers offering the crew the job of trapping the Clamper Nests.

## Known Facilities

- Gyrogate Agency (Corporate Ring, TAMA-81): area where androids and 49th Apollonian mercs guard the astronavigation and ship logistics data banks
