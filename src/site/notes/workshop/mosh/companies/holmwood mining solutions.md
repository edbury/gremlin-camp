# Holmwood Mining Solutions

Holmwood Mining Solutions is a company with an investment in the Sonata Survey Colony on the moon Carpathia II.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Echoes of Eden Prime\|Echoes of Eden Prime]]: client in the Moonbase Sonata scenario, offering 500kcr to whoever learns what happened to the Sonata Survey Colony and retrieves its geological data cube.
