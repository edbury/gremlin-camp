# Continuum Company

The Continuum Company is a company that runs science station X-3 on an asteroid and claims ownership of its discoveries.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Station Xeno\|Station Xeno]]: owner of research station X-3, the module's location, and of all discoveries made there.

## Known Products

- Tube-grown bio-androids: experimental bio-androids grown by a subset of the company, such as Vania

## Known Facilities

- Research Station X-3 (Asteroid N-Zero): science station where a quarantine breach occurred
