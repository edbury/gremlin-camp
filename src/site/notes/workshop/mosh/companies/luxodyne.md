# Luxodyne

Luxodyne is a corporation that calls itself number one in luxury items, rentals and leisure events.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Farpoint Station\|Farpoint Station]]: the luxury corporation whose station, Farpoint, is the subject of the in-world advert.

## Known Products

- LuxodyneNewsNet: subscription news service for station chit chat
- Luxodyne deep-sleep pods: custom-tailored pods offered for spa treatments (Oasis Station)

## Known Facilities

- Farpoint Station (far edge of civilized space): luxury station with shops, clubs, gardens, hospitals and lodging, now semi-abandoned
