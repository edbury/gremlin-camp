# ARC

ARC is an interstellar cruise operator whose brochure advertises its ships, the Nebula Spa, dining and activities.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/ARC Cruises\|ARC Cruises]]: the cruise line whose in-world brochure is the product.

## Known Products

- MagLock Di-Polar Thrusters: patented ship thrusters

## Known Facilities

- The Nebula Spa (aboard ARC ships): spa with included treatments and a 5kcr per day mandatory donation
