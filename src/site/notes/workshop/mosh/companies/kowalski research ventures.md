# Kowalski Research Ventures

Kowalski Research Ventures is a company preparing to study the regrowth of Pavel Theta's flora and monetize any findings.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Burying Grounds on Pavel Theta\|Burying Grounds on Pavel Theta]]: employer of the crew sent to set up base camp ahead of its survey team on a planet at its final terraforming stage.
