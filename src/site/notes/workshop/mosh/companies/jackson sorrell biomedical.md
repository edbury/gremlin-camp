# Jackson Sorrell BioMedical

Jackson Sorrell BioMedical is a company that runs trials of new drugs it hopes to bring to market, usually out of X Class ports.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Moonlit Medicine\|Moonlit Medicine]]: runs the paid drug trials the crew can sign up for.

## Known Products

- Charlie V3: healing accelerant
- Romeo V2: antiparasitic drug against lung worms
- Zulu V1: mental inhibitor meant to stop people lying, tested through interrogations by Jackson Sorrell's private police
- Hotel V4: performance enhancer for dock workers commissioned by Welland Shipping, monitored by Jackson Sorrell
