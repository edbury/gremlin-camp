# Knuckles Co

Knuckles Co is a highly trained security-for-hire corporation.

## Known Products

- Gargoyle Team: exterior perimeter and security detail
- Mole Team: infiltration and uprising squashers
- Bomb Team: finds and eliminates explosive threats near or far from the protected location
- Sound Team: LRAD-equipped team for crowd control, perimeter keeping and shutting down protests
- Burner Team: flamethrowers and grenades
- Gatecrash Team: reconnaissance and extraction team
- Collections Team: locates and collects what belongs to the client
- Terminations Team: takes out bad actors and dangerous folk
