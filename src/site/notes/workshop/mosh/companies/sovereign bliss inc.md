# Sovereign Bliss Inc.

Sovereign Bliss Inc. is a luxury goods corp and a power player in its sector that once controlled the aquatic exports of the planet Thunziter 5.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Hoss\|Hoss]]: the luxury goods corp driven off Thunziter 5 by a labor uprising.

## Known Facilities

- Sovereign Bliss Satellite Administration Office (Nerrivick derrick, Thunziter 5): former corporate office, abandoned and guarded by microturrets
- Enki derrick refinery (Thunziter 5): refinery built by Sovereign Bliss, destroyed with the derrick
