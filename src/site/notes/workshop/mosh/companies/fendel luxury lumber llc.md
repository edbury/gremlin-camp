# Fendel Luxury Lumber LLC

Fendel Luxury Lumber LLC, called "Falk" by most employees, is the company that has agriformed at least four planets, including Hylomaru 4, which it turned into a planet-wide tree farm.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Hey Sawdust!\|Hey Sawdust!]]: the employer that agriformed Hylomaru 4 into a tree farm and works the zine's lumber cutters.

## Known Facilities

- Hylomaru 4 (Finer Goods Production System): planet agriformed into a tree farm
- Polar industrial hubs and space elevators (Hylomaru 4): lumber processing and shipping, guarded by hostile security forces
