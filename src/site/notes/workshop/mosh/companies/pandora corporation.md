# Pandora Corporation

The Pandora Corporation produces vat grown exotic livestock, the primary form of income on Pandora Zephius VI.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Dissident Whispers\|Dissident Whispers]]: in Pandora's Hunt, owner of the vat-growing facility where a creation released by eco-terrorists stalks the halls and the crew is hired to neutralize it.

## Known Products

- Vat grown exotic livestock: creatures grown in vats, pitched by holograms on the facility sales floor

## Known Facilities

- Pandora creation facility (Pandora Zephius VI): growth vats, labs and a sales floor, where a released creation called the Prey stalks the halls
