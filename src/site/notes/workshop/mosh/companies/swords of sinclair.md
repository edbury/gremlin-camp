# Swords of Sinclair

The Swords of Sinclair are a former confederation of rimspace gangs, bought out by Sinclair Productions Inc. nearly 40 years ago, and now an officially licensed PMC in several systems.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/The Bloodfields at Blackstar Station\|The Bloodfields at Blackstar Station]]: Wren Sinclair's private army, which pulls ships from hyperspace, sends their crews into the Bloodfields and enforces on the station.
