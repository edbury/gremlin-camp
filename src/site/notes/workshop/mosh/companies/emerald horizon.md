# Emerald Horizon

Emerald Horizon is a megacorp dedicated to chartering worlds which lack suitable living conditions, whose employees often sign lifetime contracts.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Dissident Whispers\|Dissident Whispers]]: in the adventure Emerald Horizon, the megacorp running the undersea terraforming facility where the crew takes temp work.

## Known Facilities

- Terraforming facility (ocean world, deep under an acidic ocean): one of many facilities converting the acidic oceans into a salt water fishery, each filtering ten thousand gallons a day
