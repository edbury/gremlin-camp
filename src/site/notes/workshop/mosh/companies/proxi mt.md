# Proxi Marchandises Technologiques

Proxi Marchandises Technologiques (Proxi M.T., PMT) is one of the Coreworlds' largest Android manufacturing companies and a Rimspace Android trader. It was the first to negotiate with the Android Rights Association (ARA).

## Subsidiaries and Other Names

- PMT Merchant Guild: buys Androids from systems that do not comply with ARA guidelines and ships them to "free" stations
- Human Science Department: department that publishes the Sentience Assessment Procedure

## Known Products

- Risk and Asset Management Accountant (RAMA), PMT-C-GRA-19-5c: flagship Android line, protecting company assets
- Timber Extraction Specialist ("The Lumberjack"), PMT-SR-ExBois: lumberjack Android programmed to lead a team
- Atmospheric Compatibility Advisor, PMT-C-CA-06: Android for toxic atmospheres and vacuum, sold as Installation Androids
- Sedentary Surveillance Security Guard, PMT-ASS-ah3: security unit Android
- Personal Assistant to a Volcanic Geologist ("Phoenix"), PMT-AP-VUL9-01: heat-resistant assistant Android
- Spaceship Exterior Servicing Technician ("Ship Tech"), PMT-TEEVS-43b: hull maintenance Android
- Zoological Companion Caregiver ("Field Medic"), PMT-AZ05: pet caregiver Android, ZCC line
- Maternal Psychological Support Counselor ("Mombot"), PMT-PSM-8000: counselor Android, PSM line
- Customer Traffic Director for Mega Retail Centers ("Pilot"), PMT-DT-CGS-3y2: learn-by-doing Android
- Moral and motivational guide ("Mr. Brightside"), PMT-GMM-x32: optimism Android
- Proxi M.T. kitchenware: kitchen equipment supplied with the Mombot
- Sentience Assessment Procedure (SAP): portable human/android triage toolkit from the Human Science Department

## Known Facilities
