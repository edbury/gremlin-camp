# Xenos Unlimited

Xenos Unlimited is a bioengineering conglomerate that knows of the Pestilence and wants to study it.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/The Earth Above\|The Earth Above]]: possible patron hiring the crew to capture the Pestilence for study, and maker of the Camp Boss Androids.

- Camp Boss Model 72 Android: combat ready android designed to protect workers from outside threats
