# XeroCorp

XeroCorp is a corporation on [[workshop/mosh/Carnath System\|Carnath]] specializing in security and military R&D.

## Subsidiaries and Other Names

- XeroTech: research branch on Carnath
- XeroArms: military security division on Carnath

## Known Products

- XeroBrand Ampheta-Chews: branded stimulant chews
- XeroCoffee: coffee brand with a loyalty card
- Randy's Ready Meals: ready meal brand

## Known Vessels

- Stealth Raider: XeroCorp spacecraft

## Known Facilities

- Prison Station Echo: prefabricated prison station in the asteroids orbiting Carnath
- XeroCorp HQ: corporate headquarters on Carnath
- XeroDome: industrial dome housing XeroCorp workers on Carnath
- XeroHeights: tallest building on Carnath, with executive residences and a pleasure district
- Xero Gravity: recreation center beside the XeroDome train line on Carnath
- Apartment Block Xero-21B: housing complex on Carnath
- XeroTech Division B: weapons development division on Carnath
- XeroCorp mines: mines on Carnath
- Southern mine: former Kulakov Mining Cooperative mine on Carnath
- XeroWilderness park: employee vacation park on Proxima B
