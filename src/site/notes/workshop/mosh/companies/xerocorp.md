# XeroCorp

XeroCorp is a corporation on Carnath specializing in security and military R&D, with the subsidiaries XeroTech (research) and XeroArms (military security).

## Known Products

- Echo: XeroCorp AI Assistant System running Prison Station Echo
- AI Tracking system: advanced tracking system needed to locate Prison Station Echo in the asteroids
- XeroBrand Ampheta-Chews: branded stimulant chews
- XeroCoffee: coffee brand with a loyalty card
- Randy's Ready Meals: XeroCorp brand seen on a plastic curtain

## Known Vessels

- Stealth Raider: codelocked XeroCorp ship

## Known Facilities

- Prison Station Echo (asteroids orbiting Carnath): oldest of six near-identical prefabricated XeroCorp prisons
- XeroCorp HQ (Carnath): corporate headquarters
- XeroDome (Carnath): claustrophobic industrial dome housing XeroCorp workers
- XeroHeights (Carnath): highest building on Carnath, home to XeroCorp's elite and a pleasure district
- Xero Gravity (next to the train out of XeroDome): unofficial hang-out of XeroCorp middle management
- Apartment Block Xero-21B (Carnath): one of dozens of cookie-cutter housing complexes
- XeroTech Division B (Carnath): least profitable subsidiary, with secure servers in its vault
- XeroCorp mines (Carnath): mines where indentured workers are sent
- XeroWilderness park (Proxima B): vacation destination for employees
