# Astral Brews

Astral Brews is a takeaway beverage chain that the Warden can run as a small boutique branch of a larger corporation or as a system-spanning chain.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Astral Brews\|Astral Brews]]: the coffee-shop chain whose branch is the module's only location, where the crew start as newly onboarded employees with amnesia.

## Known Products

- Astral Brews Cerebral Recipe Chip: implant that stores the chain's Stellar Recipes as implicit memories in employees
- BrewFlux 90,000: beverage machine fed ingredients automatically from the Supply Room
- THE SPECIAL: menu item served by the booth's automated systems
- S.E.C.U.R.: security unit at the location

## Known Facilities

- Astral Brews location (Zone 45H2D; could be attached to a small moon or asteroid): coffee shop hidden away at the edge of the system, with serving booth, supply room, ingredient storage, security room and delivery bay
