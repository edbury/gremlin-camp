# PURE Refinement, Inc.

PURE Refinement, Inc. is a corporation that manufactures microchips and android components from its rhodium mining operations.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Picket Line Tango\|Picket Line Tango]]: owner of Norton Outpost that contracts the crew to break the miners' strike.

## Known Facilities

- Norton Outpost (Norton Asteroid, deep rimspace): rhodium mining colony of 100 workers under an atmospheric dome
