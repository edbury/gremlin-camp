# Luko Fin Corp

Luko Fin Corp is a mission-outsource agency that makes military and mercenary personnel disappear on missions.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Outsourced\|Outsourced]]: the antagonist agency that sends crews on missions meant to make them vanish, run from the Jiri Clovis Bunker the crew must escape.

## Known Facilities

- Jiri Clovis Bunker (privately owned dust world): massive multi-level bunker the corp works out of, with hundreds of employees
