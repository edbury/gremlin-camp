# James Hardly's Adventure Galactic

James Hardly's Adventure Galactic, also called Hardly's Galactic or the Hardly Corporation, is a rival megacorp, billed as a real estate and survivalism company, selling terraforming, colonization and survival products.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Hull Breach Vol 1\|Hull Breach Vol 1]]: company Pyramid faction trying to break into the Public Sector, with its own product advert.

## Known Products

- Carapace Survival Suit: survival suit that requires compatibility surgery
- Instant Lumber Pod: Pine Edition: time-compressed tree sapling that grows to 10m in a minute
- MindForce Injector: highly addictive caffeine hyperdose
- Yosemite SAM: hand portable anti-aircraft missile turret
- Echo Hood: helmet that projects a 3D representation of sound
- HomeSteader's Kit: paper deed, planetary coordinates and basic building supplies
- Message In A Bottle: small canister attached to a micro Jump Drive
- Moleman Power Drill: hand boring machine
- SuspendaShelter: plastic spherical sleeping shelter with adaptive camouflage
- The Meal Vat: processes any biomaterial into Survival Gruel
