# Industrial Minerals & Refinement Corp. (IM&R)

Industrial Minerals & Refinement Corp. ("The pieces of your life") is a mining and construction corp in Desdemona's Reach. It is the leading mining corp, possibly the oldest corp, and known for heavy use of explosives.
