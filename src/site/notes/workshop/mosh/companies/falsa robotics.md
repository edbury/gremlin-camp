# Falsa Robotics

Falsa Robotics is the smallest of Carnath's original Corpo-Triad and builds and supplies work and service androids.

## Known Facilities

- Falsa Dome: company dome on Carnath
- Falsa Exec Tower: former executive tower in the Falsa Dome
- Dilapidated Factory: factory in the Falsa Dome
- Falsa hab hive: former worker housing in the Falsa Dome
