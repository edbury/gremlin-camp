# Falsa Robotics

Falsa Robotics is the smallest of Carnath's original Corpo-Triad and builds and supplies work and service androids.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/The Cleaning of Prison Station Echo\|The Cleaning of Prison Station Echo]]: one of Carnath's Corpo-Triad corporations, with its own faction section in the sandbox.

## Known Products

- Falsa G robots: robot line whose broken remnants litter the Falsa Dome

## Known Facilities

- Falsa Dome (Carnath): neglected dome becoming a warzone slum
- Falsa Exec Tower (Falsa Dome): former executive tower, now a Methane Cult shrine and HQ
- Dilapidated Factory (Falsa Dome): shuttered factory from which Falsa assets are shipped to TeraTek
