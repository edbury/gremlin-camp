# The Haruthers Group

The Haruthers Group is a faction of amoral enforcers and poorly paid harvesters on the spore moon Hayden V.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/A Fistful of Spore Pods\|A Fistful of Spore Pods]]: faction of enforcers and harvesters that holds the last ship off the moon.

## Known Vessels

- Haruthers lander: snub-nosed lander with space for 9

## Known Facilities

- Abandoned supply depot (Hayden V): modular expedition supply depot bearing a faded Haruthers Group logo
