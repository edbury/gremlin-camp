# Evergreen Heavy Industries

Evergreen Heavy Industries (EHI) is the corporation that owns and runs Waystation Juniper Y31, where every permanent resident and worker is an EHI employee.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Yuletide Spacebase Massacre\|Yuletide Spacebase Massacre]]: the corporation that owns and runs Waystation Juniper Y31.

## Known Facilities

- Waystation Juniper Y31: major refueling hub with room for up to 20 freighters
- Weapons Factory (Waystation Juniper Y31): mass production of pulse rifles
