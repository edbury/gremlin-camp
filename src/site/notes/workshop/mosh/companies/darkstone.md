# Darkstone

Darkstone is a conglomerate with businesses in mining, research, higher education and disposal services.

## Subsidiaries and Other Names

- Darkstone Mining Solutions: operator of most of the mining colonies on the planetoid Jericho 475
- Darkstone For-Profit Galactic University: for-profit university that employs researchers
- Dark Stone Disposal Services: disposal service to contact if pseudo flesh begins speaking
- Dark Stone Theological Exploitation Dept.: theology research department

## Known Facilities

- Jericho 475 mining colonies: mining colonies on the planetoid Jericho 475, run by Darkstone Mining Solutions
- Darkstone research pod: research pod at the Radiant Nexus
