# Darkstone

Darkstone is a corporate name that appears as Darkstone Conglomerates, Darkstone Mining Solutions and Darkstone For-Profit Galactic University.

## Known Products

- Mother: AI that Darkstone Conglomerates sold to the Seekers 1,500 years prior

## Known Facilities

- Mining colonies (Jericho 475): most of the planetoid's mining colonies are run by Darkstone Mining Solutions
- Darkstone research pod (Radiant Nexus): research pod studying the Radiant Nexus
- Darkstone For-Profit Galactic University: university (DFPGU)
