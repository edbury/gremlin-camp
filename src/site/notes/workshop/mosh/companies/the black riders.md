# The Black Riders

The Black Riders are mercenaries from the frigid steppes of Caestus who wear blacked-out battle armor and work for Sharpe Co. in Sable.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Escape Sable\|Escape Sable]]: the mercenaries Sharpe Co. sends into Sable to evacuate the remaining VIPs, who kill everyone in their path.

## Known Vessels

- Black Rider dropships: parked in the Sable dockyard, with room for dozens of passengers
