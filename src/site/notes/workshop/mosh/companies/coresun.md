# CoreSun

CoreSun is a company whose CEO, Jack Micklus, hires the crew to retrieve data backups from an abandoned weapons blacksite and tag the Wumpus.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Wumpus Hunt\|Wumpus Hunt]]: the employer whose CEO sends the crew into the abandoned blacksite for its data backups.
