# Pantheon Security

Pantheon Security is a military megacorp running an ongoing weapons testfire on the backwater planet Öde II.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Weapons Test 23\|Weapons Test 23]]: antagonist whose testfire war machines on Öde II the crew is hired to destroy.

## Known Products

- Sweet Baby Boy (SBB): building-sized black cube war machine that drags itself on mechanical limbs and carries the B-drones in armoured slots on its back
- B-drones: small airborne drones armed with beam cannons (4d10) that recharge and repair at SBB
- Golden Brilliance Zyver: Pantheon mech suit of supervisor Gunter Iegel's own design
