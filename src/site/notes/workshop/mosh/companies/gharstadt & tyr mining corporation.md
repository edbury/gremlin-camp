# Gharstadt & Tyr Mining Corporation

Gharstadt & Tyr Mining Corporation, also called G-T Corp, is a mining corporation that funds and operates the mining of the Ghadavers of Netjeru with corporate marines and penal miners.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Graveyard of the Gods\|Graveyard of the Gods]]: the corporation that runs the Netjeru mines, fits the crew with explosive collars and sets them mining.

## Known Facilities

- Lucent (Netjeru): arcology that serves as Gharstadt and Tyr's corporate headquarters on the planet
