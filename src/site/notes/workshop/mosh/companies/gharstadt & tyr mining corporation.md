# Gharstadt & Tyr Mining Corporation

Gharstadt & Tyr Mining Corporation, also called G-T Corp, is a mining corporation that mines the Ghadavers of Netjeru alongside [[workshop/mosh/companies/adius & sons mining co\|Adius & Sons]] and fields corporate marines there.

## Known Facilities

- Habitation arcologies: eight habitation arcologies on Netjeru
- Lucent: arcology on Netjeru serving as the corporation's headquarters on the planet
