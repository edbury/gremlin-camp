# Sineto Chem Solutions

Sineto Chem Solutions is a major chemical company in the sector that fields fleets of prospectors, surveyors and claims lawyers to bottleneck and price fix raw materials.

## Known Vessels

- Hestia IV: freighter that trucks for Sineto and serves as a personnel transport

## Known Facilities

- Sineto Deep Space Factory (SDSF): city-sized factory station with Metallurgic Sciences, Bio-Tech & Pharma, Plastics and Agriculture sections and the Sineto Spike residential district; home port of the medical research vessel Chiron (J1C-IV)
- Salt mines (Dalmorus VIII): Sineto owns nearly all of them
