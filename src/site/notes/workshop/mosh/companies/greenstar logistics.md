# GreenStar Logistics

GreenStar Logistics is a Public Sector company in shipbuilding, hyperspace technology, freight and freight infrastructure.

## Known Products

- Trailblazer Drive: revolutionary drive claimed to pave a hyperspace lane in a single Jump

## Known Vessels

- Appian Way: Road Work freighter retrofitted with the Trailblazer Drive
- Cortesia: GreenStar vessel (type not stated)
- GreenStar Shuttle (or Jump Liner): passenger transport, a travel option for Rimspace salarymen

## Known Facilities

- Siesta-3, Automated Station W-900CJ-45 (orbiting a forgotten Rimspace planet): former GreenStar rest station, now the Siesta-3 Autonomous Zone anarchist commune
- Siesta-4: GreenStar automated service station
- Siesta-5: GreenStar service station
