# Takahashi Advanced Genetics

Takahashi Advanced Genetics (T.A.G.) is a megacorp dedicated to research into expanding the human genome that has fallen into questionable practices, bureaucratic corruption and 'black' science.

## Known Facilities

- Shen Kuo Research Station (Ur, a jungle moon in the Eridani system): T.A.G. research facility whose staff lost contact with HQ
- T.A.G. Outpost LV-0316: outpost the expeditionary team departed from
