# Indo-Dynamic

Indo-Dynamic is a corporation that has micronized laser technology into hand-portable cutting units, based in Laser Cove on the moon NuMiami.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Miami Laser Cutter Massacre\|Miami Laser Cutter Massacre]]: the corporation whose stolen prototype laser cutters the crew smuggles while its released convicts hunt them.

## Known Products

- 6000-SUx: prototype hand-portable laser cutter

## Known Facilities

- One Indo-Dynamic Tower (Laser Cove, NuMiami): laser research and development facility and marketing headquarters
