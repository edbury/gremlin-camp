# Indo-Dynamic

Indo-Dynamic is a corporation that has micronized laser technology into hand-portable cutting units, based in Laser Cove on the moon NuMiami.

## Known Facilities

- One Indo-Dynamic Tower: laser research and development facility and marketing headquarters in Laser Cove on NuMiami
