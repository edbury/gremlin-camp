# Czinzho Industries

Czinzho Industries ("Your partner in everything") is an old, established corp working in mining, construction and logistics that owns entire star systems and defends them with force.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/The Fat 10\|The Fat 10]]: one of the ten top corps in Desdemona's Reach profiled in the guide.
