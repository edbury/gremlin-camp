# TeraTek Amalgamate

TeraTek Amalgamate is a rising clone labour company and one of the three corporations of the Corpo-Triad that governs [[workshop/mosh/Carnath System\|Carnath]]. Run by twin CEOs Amara and Amaranth Salba, it handles most of the planet's mining operations.

## Known Facilities

- TeraTek Amalgamate Mine Works: mines on Carnath
- TeraTek Enclave: residential dome for TeraTek employees and clones on Carnath
- Double Double Helix: entertainment venue in the TeraTek Enclave
- Amaranth Tower: CEO tower in the TeraTek Enclave
- Amara Tower: CEO offices in the TeraTek Enclave
- New Clone Education Center: cloning facility in the TeraTek Enclave
- Administration and Finance Office: offices and data servers in the TeraTek Enclave
- Teratek Security: security barracks in the TeraTek Enclave
