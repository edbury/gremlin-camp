# Arkady's Outpost

Arkady's Outpost is an independent trading outpost on the outskirts of Larstown on the moon of Karth, run by its sole proprietor Arkady Labrynthe.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Arkady's Outpost\|Arkady's Outpost]]: the trading outpost that is the pamphlet's subject, whose owner offers the bounty job.

## Known Products

- Thumper: wind-up mechanical rabbit for attracting sandsquid
- Hagfish Bar: dry block of powdered hagfish that sustains 1 person for 1 day
- Bunker Rifle: triple-barreled long rifle with an axe strapped to the barrel
- Monoblade: nearly invisible katana-like blade
- Reflexive Duster: duster jacket with adaptive camouflage nanofibers
- Prospector's Special: shovel, sieve, rope and searcher shells
- Manian Sealia: drug synthesized from a Manian aquatic plant
- Delivery Service: radio-order deliveries to remote clients on Karth

## Known Facilities

- Arkady's Outpost (western edge of Larstown, Karth): independent trading outpost and shop
