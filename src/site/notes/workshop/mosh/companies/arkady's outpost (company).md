# Arkady's Outpost

Arkady's Outpost is an independent trading outpost on the outskirts of Larstown on the moon of Karth, run by its sole proprietor Arkady Labrynthe.

## Known Products

- Thumper: wind-up mechanical rabbit for attracting sandsquid (650cr)
- Hagfish Bar: dry block of powdered hagfish that sustains 1 person for 1 day (100cr)
- Bunker Rifle: triple-barreled long rifle with an axe strapped to the barrel, 2d10 DMG (7,500cr)
- Monoblade: nearly invisible katana-like blade, 3d10 DMG (5,500cr)
- Reflexive Duster: 3 AP duster jacket with adaptive camouflage nanofibers (8,000cr)
- Prospector's Special: shovel, sieve, rope and searcher shells (2,000cr)
- Manian Sealia: drug synthesized from a Manian aquatic plant (500cr)
- Handheld Radio: radio with a chance of reducing Stress while camping, resting or on shore leave (300cr)
- Imported Coffee: grants advantage on Speed Checks for 1d10 minutes (250cr)
- Mystery Box: small box tied with string (100cr)
- Delivery Service: radio-order deliveries to remote clients on Karth

## Known Facilities

- Arkady's Outpost: independent trading outpost and shop on the western edge of Larstown on Karth
