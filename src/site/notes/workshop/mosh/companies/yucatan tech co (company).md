# Yucatan Tech Co

Yucatan Tech Co is a weapons company that sells weapons and gear through its catalogs and offers weapon-armor integration services.

## Known Products

- Cryo-Gauntlets: stainless steel gauntlet that fires Zero-Capsule cryogenic cartridges
- Hola Grenades: stun grenades with Hola and Adios settings for variable damage
- My Blonde Friend: remote-controlled boomerang grappling weapon with Apollo flares
- Solar Slug Gun: laser gun recharged by a Solar Rucksack
- Proton Musket IV: weapon
- Paralysis Grenade: grenade
- Big Boom XIII: item
- Blast Visor: visor, 500cr
- Concussion Grenade: grenade, 320cr
- Miss VE's Grenade: grenade
- Laser Turret Grenade: grenade, 350cr
- Ultimate Cover Grenade: grenade
- Solar Spray Drone: drone
- My Cold Friend: item, 900cr
- Hover Mines: mines, 440cr
- Multi-Use Solar Visor: visor, 325cr
- The Narwhal: lightweight armor-piercing laser rifle
- Blaster Rifle VI: rifle, 900cr
- Shoulder Blades V: armor with a telescopic blade from each shoulder guard, 900cr
- Sleeper Gloves: gloves with concealed anaesthetic and neuroblocker gas, 350cr
- Spike Shot: knee pad and shin guard with a telescopic harpoon, 250cr
- Vibiwhip: vibrating razor-bladed whip for swarming alien creatures, 525cr
- Pepper Grenade: delayed grenade that pops three smaller explosives, 325cr
- Auto-Shield Rucksack: rucksack with a telescopic shield that deploys when danger is detected, 225cr
- BatteryPlus Laser Rifle: two-handed laser rifle with 16 shots, 800cr
- Nano-Helm: helmet with 4x sight visor and rapid-heal nanobots, 800cr
- Fireshow: fire-spitting whip, 2650cr
- The Talon: grappling whip with hydraulic talons and explosive pot shot, 450cr
- Save-A-Life Tech: low-depth brain implant behind the ear that auto-releases nanobots when immense pain is experienced
