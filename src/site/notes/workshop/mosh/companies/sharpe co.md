# Sharpe Co.

Sharpe Co. (Sharpe Mining Corporation) is an interstellar mining megacorporation that specializes in mineral extraction and processing.

## Known Products

- Sharpe Co. Clearance Code Chip: chip embedded in the comms relay of every Sharpe Co. ship that lets it move freely
- Automated Port Entry System: pings incoming traffic and automatically clears ships with a Sharpe Co. Clearance Chip for landing
- SMOCA: Sharpe Maintenance Operations Control Android

## Known Vessels

- John Henry: Sharpe Co. flagship; capital ship equipped for colony suppression
- Horsefly: Kadaar strike ship stolen from Sharpe Co.
- Sharpe Co. Hovertrain: Class IV hovertrain with a 1d10 MDMG autocannon
- Unnamed corvette: Sharpe Co. corvette that orbits Mani to enforce landing rules

## Known Facilities

- Kadaar Mining Facility (rimworld S-4813): rhodium mine where the workers revolted
- Sharpe Co. garage (Sable): former supply depot and maintenance bay
- Sharpe Co. Compound (Last Atoll, Mani): walled luxury enclave reached by monorail tram
- Aurora Diamond Facility (offshore, Mani): offshore aurora diamond mining operation served by hovertrain
- Sharpe Co. Exchange (Spaceport Docks, Mani): kiosk that exchanges credits for Baronial chits
- Tinea Weather Station (orbiting Mani): weather station commissioned to study Mani's weather patterns
