# Sharpe Co.

Sharpe Co. (Sharpe Mining Corporation) is a megacorp with dozens of outback worlds under its thumb that specializes in mineral extraction and processing.

## Known Vessels

- John Henry: capital ship, the company flagship
- Sharpe Co. Hovertrain: Class IV hovertrain

## Known Facilities

- Kadaar Mining Facility: former rhodium mine on the rimworld S-4813
- Sable: company town on S-4813
- Sharpe Co. garage: former supply depot and maintenance bay in Sable on S-4813
- Sharpe Co. Compound: walled luxury enclave in Last Atoll on Mani
- Aurora Diamond Facility: offshore aurora diamond mine on Mani
- Sharpe Co. Exchange: currency exchange kiosk at the Spaceport Docks on Mani
- Tinea Weather Station: weather station orbiting Mani
