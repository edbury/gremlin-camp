# De Witt Holdings

De Witt Holdings is a megacorp with a high investment in research and colony projects in the Gnion system.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/The View at the End of Time\|The View at the End of Time]]: the megacorp that built the wormhole portal and sends the crew through it.

## Known Facilities

- The Conduit (stable orbit between Gnion's twin suns): space platform that draws power from the stars to open a stable wormhole
