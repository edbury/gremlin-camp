# The Euthanian Minerals Company

The Euthanian Minerals Company is a company prospecting on the desert planet Euthania.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Under the Dunes\|Under the Dunes]]: the employer that sends the crew as an expeditionary force to scope out a metallic deposit.
