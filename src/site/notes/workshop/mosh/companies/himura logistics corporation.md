# Himura Logistics Corporation

Himura Logistics Corporation operates Himura Station and focuses on processing void leviathan Harvest for resale to other entities.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/The Void Leviathan Field Manual\|The Void Leviathan Field Manual]]: operator of Himura Station, the whaling hub where crews sell Harvest.

## Known Facilities

- Himura Station: whaling hub treated as a B-Class Port, mostly made up of Himura Logistics' large Harvest processing factories, with corporate offices for other companies
