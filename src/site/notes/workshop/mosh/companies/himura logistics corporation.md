# Himura Logistics Corporation

Himura Logistics Corporation operates Himura Station and focuses on processing void leviathan Harvest for resale to other entities.

## Known Facilities

- Himura Station: mobile whaling hub and B-Class Port in Leviathan territory, made up mostly of Himura Logistics' Harvest processing factories, with corporate offices for other companies
