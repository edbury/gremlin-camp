# MAUVEARTE LLC

MAUVEARTE LLC, colloquially known as Mauve, is a corporation that provides services across the art and antiques trade, including curation and investment.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Mauve-Arte\|Mauve-Arte]]: the art-trade corporation whose auction catalogue is the product.

## Known Products

- MAUVE ARTE Quarterly Catalog #4.23: the company's art auction catalogue
- MAUVE|BLACK edition: confidential catalogue edition that corporate executives base their investment decisions on
