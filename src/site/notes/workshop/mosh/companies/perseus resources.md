# Perseus Resources

Perseus Resources, also called Perseus Corporation, is the corporation whose scrip is the currency of the Persei 19 mining station.

## Known Products

- Perseus scrip: corporate currency used on Persei 19 station, paid out in cheap Perseus-made alloy coins
