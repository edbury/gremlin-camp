# ALCOR Fuel & Services

ALCOR Fuel & Services is a subsidiary of Shikewara-Tarmec Corp. that runs more than a hundred ALCOR refuelling stations serving cheap fuel cores, entertainment and food to spaceship crews.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/ALCOR Station\|ALCOR Station]]: operator of the refuelling station chain that is the module's whole location.

## Known Facilities

- ALCOR Station (more than a hundred across known space): refuelling station with docking slots for minor repairs, a slow automated refuelling system, canteen, arcade and rest areas, staffed by repurposed prisoners
