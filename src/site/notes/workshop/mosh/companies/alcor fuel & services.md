# ALCOR Fuel & Services

ALCOR Fuel & Services is a subsidiary of Shikewara-Tarmec Corp. that runs more than a hundred ALCOR refuelling stations serving cheap fuel cores, entertainment and food to spaceship crews.

## Known Facilities

- ALCOR Stations: chain of more than a hundred refuelling stations across known space, with docking slots for minor repairs, canteen, arcade and rest areas
- Alcor Station: rest station, Narcissus System
