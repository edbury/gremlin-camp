# Kulakov Mining Cooperative

The Kulakov Mining Cooperative (KMC) is a worker-owned cooperative on Carnath that runs the docks and excavates areas the larger corporations consider unprofitable.

## Known Facilities

- Dock Dome: dock district at Founder's Spaceport on Carnath
