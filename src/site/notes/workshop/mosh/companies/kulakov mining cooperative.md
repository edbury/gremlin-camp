# Kulakov Mining Cooperative

The Kulakov Mining Cooperative (KMC) is a worker-owned cooperative on Carnath that runs the docks and excavates areas the larger corporations consider unprofitable.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/The Cleaning of Prison Station Echo\|The Cleaning of Prison Station Echo]]: worker-owned Carnath faction with its own section, controlling the docks.

## Known Facilities

- Dock Dome (Carnath): the KMC's last bastion, controlling everything that passes through Founder's Spaceport
