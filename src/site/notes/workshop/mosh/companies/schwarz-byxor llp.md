# Schwarz-Byxor LLP

Schwarz-Byxor LLP is an aerospace and military megacorp with its own private army and fleet that builds and sells mecha-hardware to both sides of wars.

## Known Products

- Lance series: mech line including the Scout Lance and Strike Lance
- Sea Lance: mech purpose-built for the civil war on Vincennes, suited to ocean or high-pressure worlds (Combat Laser 2d10 MDMG, 210Mcr, 3 cores, 13 HP per core, 7 AP; in water or pressures greater than 5 atmospheres, +5 to AP and Combat)

## Known Facilities

- Mobile shipyards: mobile mech shipyards that travel between warzones
