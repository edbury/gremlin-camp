# Schwarz-Byxor LLP

Schwarz-Byxor LLP is an aerospace and military megacorp with its own private army and fleet that builds and sells mecha-hardware to both sides of wars.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Mechs in the City\|Mechs in the City]]: aerospace and military megacorp with its own mech faction entry, maker of the Lance mech series.

## Known Products

- Lance series: mech line including the Scout Lance and Strike Lance
- Sea Lance: mech purpose-built for the civil war on Vincennes, suited to ocean or high-pressure worlds

## Known Facilities

- Mobile shipyards: vast shipyards flown from warzone to warzone to build mecha-hardware where it will be sold
