# Moroi Technologies

Moroi Technologies, also called Moroi Technology Lab (MTL), is a company that hires the crew as contractors for its deep ocean research facility.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Decagone\|Decagone]]: the crew's employer and owner of the deep ocean research facility caught in time loops.

## Known Products

- Moroi Tech NanoDefib: fully automated single-use defibrillator that replaces a recipient's entire blood supply

## Known Facilities

- Deep ocean research facility (ocean floor): research lab reached by elevator, where an experiment loops time every 10 minutes
