# Moroi Technologies

Moroi Technologies, also called Moroi Technology Lab (MTL), is a company that conducts research and develops technologies in defence, pharmaceuticals, long-distance space travel and agriculture.

## Known Products

- Moroi Tech NanoDefib: fully automated single-use defibrillator that replaces a recipient's entire blood supply

## Known Facilities

- Deep ocean research facility: research lab on the ocean floor
