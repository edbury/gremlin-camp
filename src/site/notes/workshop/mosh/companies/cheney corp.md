# Cheney Corp

Cheney Corp is the company that owns the Vespucci and most assets on board. Products in the same module are credited to Cheney Industries; the text doesn't say whether this is the same company.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/SIS TR A Rare Geometry\|SIS TR A Rare Geometry]]: owns the silent exploration cruiser Vespucci 7 and pays the rewards for rescuing its crew.

## Known Products

- Gauntlet 3 (credited to Cheney Industries): glove with a foldable electronic interface and front-facing projector
- Auger Grenade (credited to Cheney Industries): heavy duty grenade that drills into walls, floors and ceilings before detonating

## Known Vessels

- Vespucci 7: exploration cruiser
