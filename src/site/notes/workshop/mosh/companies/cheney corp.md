# Cheney Corp

Cheney Corp is a corporation that owns the Vespucci 7, the seventh in a series of exploration craft.

## Subsidiaries and Other Names

- Cheney Industries: manufacturer of equipment and weapons

## Known Products

- Gauntlet 3: glove with a foldable electronic interface and front-facing projector
- Auger Grenade: heavy duty grenade that drills into walls, floors and ceilings before detonating, 3d10 DMG

## Known Vessels

- Vespucci 7: exploration cruiser
