# Abattoir Family Co.

Abattoir Family Co. is a Public Sector company selling office supply and IP retention products and engaging in espionage.

## Known Products

- Blood Loader: handheld device that extracts blood and prints crystal shapes (750cr)
- Corporate Obelisk: enshrined ship module with an instantaneous communication hardline to other attuned Obelisks (5mcr)
- Fixer Drone: corporate android enforcer in a spray can (12kcr, requires corporate cosign)
- O2-Shredder Spike: serrated corkscrew that extracts oxygen from a human corpse into a tank (600cr)
- Optographic Probe: probe that projects what a deceased target saw before death (5,000cr)
- Contractor's Servos: implanted servos that mechanically limit behavior and forbid mutiny (10kcr)
- Cryo Gun: gun that flash freezes corpses for field preservation (1,500cr)
- BioSecurity Key Reclaimer: autonomous briefcase that removes and stores corporate-logged fingers and eyes (3,500cr)
- Geko Suit: vaccsuit with an auto-amputation function (1,800cr)
- IP Recuperation Table: med bay installation that extracts 2 Skill chips from a corpse (1bcr)

## Known Facilities

- Abattoir Family Theme Station: theme station with sales displays and amusements
