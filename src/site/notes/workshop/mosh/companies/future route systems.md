# Future Route Systems

Future Route Systems is a robot manufacturer, particularly of high-end autonomous combat drones that skirt the illegality of full AI robotic soldiers in the sector.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Why Robots\|Why Robots]]: owner of the robotics facility whose internal scans have detected signs of deliberate hacking in its production lines' network.

## Known Products

- Remote combat drone (RCD): autonomous combat drone

## Known Facilities

- Future Route Systems facility (location not stated): building with a glass-fronted entrance lobby, assembly lines and storage bays for drones
