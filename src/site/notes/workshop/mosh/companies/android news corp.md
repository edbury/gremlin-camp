# Android News Corp

Android News Corp (also called Android Press Corp) is a news outlet billed as the only news for androids by androids, transmitted directly into memory discs.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/ANIFORMS\|ANIFORMS]]: in-world publisher of the newsletter, which assigned its reporters to the aniforming story.
