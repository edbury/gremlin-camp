# Tokugawa Enterprises

Tokugawa Enterprises is a company with a fleet of spacecraft and a science department, led by CEO Hiroshi Nakamura.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/USS Scorpio Please Respond\|USS Scorpio Please Respond]]: the employer that hires the crew to recover the research vessel's cargo, and whose protocol docks their ship to it.

## Known Products

- Global Mesh Emergency Protocol (G.M.E.P): emergency protocol in every Tokugawa spacecraft that takes control of an assisting ship and docks it to a vessel in need
