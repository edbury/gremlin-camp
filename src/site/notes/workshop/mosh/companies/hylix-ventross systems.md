# Hylix-Ventross Systems

Hylix-Ventross Systems is a Public Sector company in communications, technology and robotics development that runs human experiments in its labs; Graveyard of the Gods uses it as Hylix-Ventross Labs with permission from Hull Breach Vol. 1.

## Known Products

- Skulker-Fresnel Resonator: prototype that transmits and reassembles matter at interstellar distances
- Hylix-Ventross stress doll: stress doll with collectable faces
- Hylix-Ventross explosive collars: standard collars fitted to all newcomers on Netjeru to ensure cooperation

## Known Facilities

- Hylix-Ventross HQ: houses the Dr. Skulker Memorial File Server
- Hylix-Ventross anomaly testing labs: research base that tests anomalous and hazardous artifacts on human subjects (Residue Processing)
- Clamrest (planet Smythe, System of Joy): town owned and managed by Hylix-Ventross
- Borealis (Netjeru): top-secret research lab arcology where the most important samples and artifacts from digs end up
- Research facility Delta: corporate research facility hit by a Class-3 xenobiological exposure incident (Agent Class)
