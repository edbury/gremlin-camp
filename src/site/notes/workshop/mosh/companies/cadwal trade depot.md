# Cadwal Trade Depot

The Cadwal Trade Depot is a fleet of merchant ships that travel across the stars, avoiding local tax laws by never selling their wares while docked.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Gear for a Spacefarer\|Gear for a Spacefarer]]: the merchant fleet whose gear list and contracts table make up the pamphlet.

## Known Vessels

- CTD Lucille: C Class Port with a mostly android staff
- CTD Isabella: fleet ship that stopped transmitting in the Rose Asteroid Belt
