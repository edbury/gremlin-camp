# Adius & Sons Mining Co.

Adius & Sons Mining Co. is a mining company founded by the Adius family that discovered the moon Netjeru while searching for a way to the Galactic Core.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Graveyard of the Gods\|Graveyard of the Gods]]: the mining company that discovered Netjeru and mines it alongside G-T Corp; its last remaining founder, CEO Richard Adius, gives the crew jobs.
