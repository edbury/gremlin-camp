# Decker-Yun

Decker-Yun is a corp tagged for mining, logistics and population work that began in logistics and moved into population work after its ships found vast water ice clusters, and is described as merciless toward its people.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/The Fat 10\|The Fat 10]]: one of the ten top corps in Desdemona's Reach profiled in the guide.
