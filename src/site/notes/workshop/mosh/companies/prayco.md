# PrayCo

PrayCo is a private prisoner reformation company. It also runs an orbital megachurch.

## Subsidiaries and Other Names

- PrayCo Correctional Division: correctional division that keeps inmate records

## Known Products

- PrayCo brand coloring books: Dante's Adventures, Martyrs A-Z and Colors of the Crusades

## Known Vessels

- Charity: penal transport ship

## Known Facilities

- PrayCo private reform colony: prison colony
- Fallen Angel Reform School: reform school
- PrayCo Worship Complex: orbital megachurch
- Former Prayco factory: computer chip factory and abbey
