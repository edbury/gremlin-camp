# PrayCo

PrayCo is a religious-branded company that runs private prisoner reformation and an orbital megachurch in Anodyne Printware's books and, in The Black Heart of Paradise (which The Drain credits as PrayCo's source), runs a computer chip factory and an abbey of nuns.

## Known Products

- Tinsel halo: PrayCo battle uniform issued to prisoners
- PrayCo brand coloring books: Dante's Adventures, Martyrs A-Z and Colors of the Crusades
- Motherchips: prototype computer chips from the Prayco factory, one-of-a-kind after its destruction
- Sinlessly: Prayco software installed on tech that crafts prayers, with a 4cr per minute surcharge

## Known Vessels

- Charity: penal transport ship

## Known Facilities

- PrayCo private reform colony: prison colony whose prisoners are drafted into holy war
- Fallen Angel Reform School: PrayCo reform institution whose prisoners wear tinsel halos
- PrayCo Worship Complex: garish orbital megachurch run by Pastor Franklin
- Prayco factory: computer chip factory destroyed by a fire that killed 120 workers
