# Perigee Corporation

Perigee Corporation is a planetary-scale development company that owns and operates the ocean world Meridian as a luxury planet destination.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/The Oceans are Endless on Meridian\|The Oceans are Endless on Meridian]]: owner and operator of Meridian and the PCs' employer through a temporary workforce initiative to complete Harbor Blue.

## Known Products

- Planets by Perigee: terraforming brand
- Perigee Superstructurals: space infrastructure brand for habitat clusters, launch tether networks and worldhouses
- Perigen: agriculture, medicine and biotechnology research brand
- Orbital Passenger Capsule PRS-828 "Scylla" Model B: Perigee Superstructurals capsule for the Meridian Space Elevator

## Known Vessels

- Beagle: Perigee Science Vessel

## Known Facilities

- Meridian (KT-10924 c): high-gravity super-Earth ocean world operated as a luxury planet destination
- Harbor Blue (Meridian): artificial island on colossal pylons, slated to open as a vacation resort for wealthy tourists
- Meridian Space Elevator (Meridian): space elevator served by Perigee passenger capsules
- Observatory (Harbor Blue, Meridian): where Perigee astronomers search for new planets to terraform
