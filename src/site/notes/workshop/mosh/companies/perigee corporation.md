# Perigee Corporation

Perigee Corporation is a planetary-scale industry company. It owns and operates the ocean world Meridian as a luxury planet destination.

## Subsidiaries and Other Names

- Planets by Perigee: terraforming brand that engineers colony environments
- Perigee Superstructurals: space infrastructure brand for habitat clusters, launch tether networks and worldhouses
- Perigen: research brand in agriculture, medicine and biotechnology

## Known Vessels

- Beagle: Perigee Science Vessel

## Known Facilities

- Meridian: luxury ocean planet destination, designated KT-10924 c
- Harbor Blue: artificial island resort on Meridian
- Meridian Space Elevator: space elevator on Meridian
- Observatory: astronomical observatory on Harbor Blue, Meridian
