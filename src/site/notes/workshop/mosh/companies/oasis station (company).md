# Oasis Station

Oasis Station is a deep-space relaxation spa located between Prion II and the Glasian Belt.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Oasis Station\|Oasis Station]]: the deep-space spa business whose in-world brochure is the product.

## Known Products

- DeepMassage: proprietary subdermal massage technology using nanoscopic filaments
- Accu-Puncture: patented Autonomous Arm system that applies up to 100 needles in under 60 seconds
- Whole-body cryotherapy: treatments sold for pain, parasites, weight loss and dementia
- Overnight sensory deprivation: new overnight service

## Known Facilities

- Oasis Station (between Prion II and the Glasian Belt): spa station with facials, sauna and steam room, and the LuxoDine on-station fine dining establishment
