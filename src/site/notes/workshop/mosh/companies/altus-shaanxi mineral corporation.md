# Altus-Shaanxi Mineral Corporation

The Altus-Shaanxi Mineral Corporation (ASMC) is a mining company that has mined minerals for almost a century, staking claims on asteroids and planetoids other mineral-extraction companies didn't dare take. It is also called the Altus-Shaanxi Mining Company.

## Known Facilities

- Station 324: ASMC mining station on a planetoid
