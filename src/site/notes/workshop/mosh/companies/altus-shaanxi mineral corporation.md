# Altus-Shaanxi Mineral Corporation

The Altus-Shaanxi Mineral Corporation (ASMC) is a mining company that has mined minerals for almost a century, staking claims on high-risk asteroids and planetoids.

Role in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Altus-Shaanxi Mining Company\|Altus-Shaanxi Mining Company]]: the mining company whose orientation handout is the product and which runs Station 324.

## Known Products

- ASMC dedicated currency: company currency issued to staff

## Known Facilities

- Station 324 (a planetoid): ASMC mining station with a Security Pillar entrance, mining tunnels and habs
