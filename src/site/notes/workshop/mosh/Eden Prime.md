# Eden Prime

No source names a region; the label is unofficial. Molgannard's [[workshop/mosh/modules/Echoes of Eden Prime\|Echoes of Eden Prime]] and [[workshop/mosh/modules/The Sins of Eden Prime\|The Sins of Eden Prime]] share the Seekers, the Church of the Holy Triumvirate and Darkstone.

## Locations

- Eden Prime: jungle colony founded by the Seekers and their ship AI, Mother
- Moonbase Sonata: survey colony on Carpathia II, moon of the gas giant Carpathia I
- The Prophet's Mesa: backwater planet dotted with mesas
- The Radiant Nexus: anomalous point of light on the far rim of known space

## Factions

- Darkstone
- Church of the Holy Triumvirate
- Holmwood Mining Solutions
