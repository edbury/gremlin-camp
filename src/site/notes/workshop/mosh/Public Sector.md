# Public Sector

Anodyne Printware's sector "dominated by the Publico megacorporation," defined in [[workshop/mosh/modules/Hull Breach Vol 1\|Hull Breach Vol. 1]].

## Systems

- Love System (corespace): Lord Wesley's Rise (hub station for corespace campaigns), Hodder Forest Reserve Habitat, The Silver Noose
- Joy System (rimspace): Fairpoint Rock, Smythe's Planet, Siesta-3 (hub station and the Siesta-3 Autonomous Zone), Mandella IV
