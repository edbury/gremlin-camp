# Ferrier's Fen

The folk of [[Marshedge\|Marshedge]] collect wild rice and herbs from Ferrier's Fen, but no one goes too deep. The scaly, slimy, lizard-like suarachan who live there drag fools away to sacrifice to their foul gods.

Something has made the suarachan bold. [[workshop/stonetop/characters/Nitza the Ranger\|Nitza]] lost her favorite bow to it, and it left behind a mysterious trail of slime.
