# The Steplands

> The Hillfolk roam the rugged Steplands and the Flats, and revile Gordin's Delve.

> The Steplands are a rugged wilderness, home to the nomadic Hillfolk: horselords and shepherds, fierce and barbaric to outsiders. They trade horses, wool, and sometimes salt from the south, but consider [[Gordin's Delve\|Gordin's Delve]] corrupt for prying sacred metals from the earth.
>
> The Steplands are dotted with ancient burial mounds. Unlike most ruins, these are man-sized and less sophisticated than those of the Makers. The Hillfolk shun them and warn all travelers away.
>
> The Steplands hide two great terrors: Blackwater Lake (to the west) and Three-Coven Lake (to the east). The Hillfolk wisely keep their distance, but the few who have dared approach the lakes report ancient ruins on the shores, in the surrounding cliffs, and even beneath the waters.
