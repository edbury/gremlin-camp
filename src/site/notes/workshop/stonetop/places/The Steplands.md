# The Steplands

The Steplands are a rugged wilderness, home to the nomadic Hillfolk: horselords and shepherds, fierce and barbaric to outsiders. The Hillfolk trade horses, wool and sometimes salt from the south. They revile [[Gordin's Delve\|Gordin's Delve]] for prying sacred metals from the earth. Bands of Hillfolk come north onto the Flats to hunt.

Ancient burial mounds dot the Steplands. Unlike most ruins, these are man-sized and simpler than the Makers' work. The Hillfolk shun them and warn travelers away.

The Steplands hide two great terrors: Blackwater Lake to the west and Three-Coven Lake to the east. The Hillfolk keep their distance. The few who have approached the lakes report ancient ruins on the shores, in the surrounding cliffs and even beneath the waters.
