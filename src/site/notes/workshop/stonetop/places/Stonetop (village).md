# Stonetop (Village)

Stonetop is an isolated village of around 300 folk near the edge of the known world. It's poor, but people look out for each other here. Everyone is expected to pull their weight, and few think badly of those who suffer misfortune.

The village sits on a bluff overlooking the Great Wood, built on the ruins of the Makers: an ancient cistern, the crumbling Old Wall and some old foundations. By a compact with the Forest Folk, villagers never cut down a living tree. They gather fallen wood, and send folk northwest to the Foothills when they need timber. No one has seen the Forest Folk in ten years.

There's no government, just leaders, and everyone knows who they are: the wise, the cunning, the brave. Every able-bodied adult keeps a spear, a round wooden shield and maybe a bow, and folk take turns manning the three watchtowers each night.

Most buildings are low and squat, built of stone scavenged from the Old Wall and thatched with barley straw. Families pass their homes down, with two or three generations living together. The waist-high Ringwall circles the village. Houses outside it are newer, built by younger families and recent arrivals.

Most folk farm barley, oats, beans and potatoes in the fields that stretch out to the Old Wall. Beyond it, the bitter grasses of the Flats choke out anything planted there. Families keep goats, chickens, sheep and pigs. The only full-time tradesfolk are a smith, a tanner, a midwife, a publican and a potter. The village owns two horses in common, a couple of plows, a pair of carts and a wagon from [[Marshedge\|Marshedge]].

Sane folk shun lakes, rivers and other deep water. Death dwells there, and everyone knows it.

## The Stone

The Stone stands well over fifty feet tall, etched with faded runes, and seems older even than the Maker ruins. Storms roll in from the southeast through spring and summer, and lightning strikes the Stone again and again. The locals barely notice.

## The Granary

Food for winter is stored in the Granary. Everyone contributes and everyone shares.

## The Public House

The Public House is the largest building in the village, where everyone gathers after sundown to drink and talk. Travelers can sleep on the common room floor, and the village's two horses live in its small stable.

## The Pavilion of the Gods

Stonetop has no temple, but the Pavilion of the Gods holds shrines to Helior the Daybringer, Danu the Earth Mother, Aratis the Lawkeeper, and Tor the Rain-Maker, Thunderhead, Slayer-of-Beasts. Tor is the most popular and is tied to the Stone itself, but all four get due respect. Helior's shrine has the place of highest honor. Aratis's shrine is newly established, cramped and spare.

## The Cistern

Rainwater, snowmelt and water hauled up from the Stream fill a huge, ancient cistern that carries the village through the dry months. The spirits of townsfolk who fell into it haunt the Chronicle.

## The Stream

The Stream runs along the base of the bluff and gives fresh water to anyone willing to haul buckets. It sometimes dries up at the height of summer. Villagers fear shallow running water less than the deep stuff, but most agree the Stream is haunted by a pair of ghostly twins.

## The Old Wall

The Old Wall surrounds Stonetop about a mile out from the Stone, at the end of the West Road. It was once a massive rampart, now tumbled and buried by the ages. Villagers dig stone out of it when they need to build. Now and then a scholar from the south comes to study it, but few can stand the conditions for long.

The Chronicle, the vault where [[workshop/stonetop/characters/Odval the Judge\|Odval]] lives and keeps his records, sits on the outskirts near the Old Wall.

## The Glassworks

[[workshop/stonetop/characters/Eirian the Lightbearer\|Eirian]]'s glassworks is a small shop in the inner ring of town, and one of the village's original buildings.
