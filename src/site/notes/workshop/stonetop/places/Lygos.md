# Lygos

Lygos is one of the more civilized towns of the arid south, far from Stonetop. Trade runs steadily between Lygos and [[Marshedge\|Marshedge]].

## People

- Gorhan: taught [[workshop/stonetop/characters/Nitza the Ranger\|Nitza]] to read and write in her youth.
- [[workshop/stonetop/characters/Nitza the Ranger\|Nitza the Ranger]].
- Spiro, who came to [[workshop/stonetop/places/Stonetop (village)\|Stonetop]] with [[workshop/stonetop/characters/Ozbeg the Seeker\|Ozbeg]] twenty years ago.
- Tovia: knows something about the suarachan of [[workshop/stonetop/places/Ferrier's Fen\|Ferrier's Fen]], if Nitza can only convince them to talk.
