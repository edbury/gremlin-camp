# Lygos

> There's no empire, no kings or nobles—at least not around here. More "civilized" towns and cities lie farther to the south.

> The plains of the South Manmarch are sparsely inhabited, by a few tribes of nomads hunting aurochs herds. But trade is steady between [[Marshedge\|Marshedge]] and Lygos and the other towns of the arid south.
