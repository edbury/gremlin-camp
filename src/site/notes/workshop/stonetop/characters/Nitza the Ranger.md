# Nitza the Ranger

> Your true home is out there. Away from the Old Roads, in the wild places, where you've faced storm and beast alike. But unknown forces are at work beyond the Ringwall, and you fear for your kith and kin. These are strange times. Guide them, ranger, and keep them safe when darkness falls.

## Vitals

Nitza is *long in the tooth*, *compact & sturdy* and *well-groomed*, with a *sing-song voice*.

- Pronouns: she/her
- Origin: [[workshop/stonetop/places/Lygos\|Lygos]]
- Instinct: Tenacity, to be stubborn, to persist
- Damage: d8
- HP: max 18

She came to Stonetop alone six years ago, wanting to connect with nature and find some peace and quiet. She can read and write, taught by [[workshop/stonetop/places/Lygos#People\|Gorhan]] in Lygos in her youth.

## Background: Beast-bonded

> You grew up civilized, but your soul is bound to a beast of the wild. You're closer to it than to any man or woman. How did this bond come about? How long ago?
>
> When you focus on your animal companion for a few moments, you can use any of the actions you've marked below, no matter the distance between you. Mark 1 action at 1st level, then another at 3rd, 5th, 7th, and 9th.

- [x] Gauge its distance and direction from you
- [ ] Call it back to your side
- [ ] Sense its emotional state
- [ ] Get a brief impression of what it senses
- [ ] Lend it your strength: lose 1d6 HP, and it regains an equal amount

Nitza's companion is Gertrude, an opossum. Six years ago, when Nitza first arrived in Stonetop, she found Gertrude injured on her first foray into the woods.

## Special Possessions

Nitza contributes to the village by hunting, bringing in pelts and meat.

- Composite bow (*far*, +1 damage, *x piercing*; low ammo, all out)
- Hideouts (3 uses): expend a use to have a well-stocked, safe shelter nearby; GM can veto.
- Hounds, 2-3 followers (*trackers*, *keen-nosed*, *fast*); HP 6; Damage d6 (*hand*, *grabby*); Instinct: to give chase; Cost: training.

## Moves

### Home on the Range

When a journey requires you to Defy Danger or Struggle as One, treat a 6- as a 7-9.

### Animal Companion

You are accompanied by a beast of uncommon loyalty and cleverness. See the Animal Companion insert for details.

### A Safe Place

When you select and prepare the party's camp site, hold 1 Precaution, or 2 Precaution if you are well-versed with this area and its dangers.

If trouble finds your camp site, you can spend 1 Precaution to reveal a simple defense, warning, or trick that you prepared in advance. If you do, tell us how you knew to make that specific preparation.

## Something Wicked This Way Comes

Nitza worries about whatever's made the lizard-like suarachan of [[workshop/stonetop/places/Ferrier's Fen\|Ferrier's Fen]] so bold. She lost her favorite bow to it. It left behind a mysterious trail of slime. [[workshop/stonetop/places/Lygos#People\|Tovia]] of Lygos can tell her more, if she can only convince them.

## Connections

- Gertrude, her opossum companion, adores [[workshop/stonetop/characters/Eirian the Lightbearer\|Eirian]] and is terrified of [[workshop/stonetop/places/Stonetop (village)#Residents\|Teagan]].
- Teagan has a lot to learn from Nitza.
- [[workshop/stonetop/places/Lygos#People\|Gorhan]] taught her to read and write in her youth.
- [[workshop/stonetop/places/Lygos#People\|Tovia]] can tell her more about the suarachan of Ferrier's Fen.
- She sometimes catches Eirian staring out at the horizon, though he's never really left.
- Eirian has shown her great beauty, including a tour of Stonetop.
