# Odval the Judge

> Look here at this little town, this candleflame in the darkness. Its very existence is an act of courage and faith. And Aratis has charged you to keep it: to settle its disputes; to chronicle its tales; to defend it from darkness and ruin. Take up your hammer, Judge. Your town needs you.

## Vitals

Odval is *showing his years* and *slim*, with *a voice that carries* and *modest clothes*.

- Pronouns: he/him
- Origin: [[workshop/stonetop/places/Barrier Pass\|Barrier Pass]]
- Instinct: Dispassion, to disregard emotion or sentiment
- Damage: d6
- HP: max 20

Aratis called him to Stonetop six months ago. His apprentice, [[workshop/stonetop/places/Stonetop (village)#Residents\|Terrance]], came with him. He can read and write, taught by his father when he was young.

## Background: Prophet

> The line of Judges was broken long ago, the Chronicle lost or fallen into ruin. Aratis has called you personally to her service through dreams, omens, and visions. Some in town resent the authority you've assumed.
>
> When you spend a few days communing with Aratis about a threat facing Stonetop or civilization as a whole, roll +WIS: on a 7+, Aratis reveals the course of action she would have you take; on a 10+, you also hold 2 Sanction. While acting on her orders, spend 1 Sanction to add +1 to a roll you just made.

Aratis called Odval to her service with a tingle in his sternum. He fell asleep and dreamt of her, and woke in Barrier Pass with his hammer. He is still working on establishing himself as Stonetop's Judge.

## Special Possessions

- Black iron maul, his symbol of authority: utterly immune to all magic (*close*, *forceful*, *awkward*, +1 damage)
- Scribe's tools: parchment, ink, pigments, vials, quills, a notebook, etc.
    - Scribing is how he earns his keep. His father, a librarian, now dead, taught him.
- Engineer's tools: rulers, tapes, rods, plumb-bobs, tripods, block & tackles, wheelbarrow, etc.
    - He uses these on the side. He got them from a passing trader on his journey to Stonetop, and no one has taught him to use them.

## Moves

### Censure

When you first denounce an individual in your presence as an agent of chaos or anathema to civilization, they pick 1:

- They are ashamed, and act accordingly
- They are doubtful, and hesitate, pause
- They are afraid, and seek to escape
- They are enraged, and lash out predictably (the next roll against them has advantage)

### Chronicler of Stonetop

When you write up detailed session notes and share them with the other players, hold +1 Diligence. You can spend 1 Diligence at any time to add +1 to a roll that you or a fellow player just made.

### Hound of Aratis

When you Seek Insight, you can always ask "What here is tainted by chaos?" for free, even on a 6-.

## The Chronicle

Odval lives in the Chronicle, a sturdy vault from the time of the Makers that bears minor magics to preserve its contents. It sits on the outskirts of town, near the [[workshop/stonetop/places/Stonetop (village)#The Old Wall\|Old Wall]], and seems to be haunted. The townspeople showed it to him. Anyone who can endure the haunting is welcome there. The spirits are townsfolk who fell into the [[workshop/stonetop/places/Stonetop (village)#The Cistern\|cistern]], and they appear at irregular times.

## The Lawkeeper

In Stonetop's [[workshop/stonetop/places/Stonetop (village)#The Pavilion of the Gods\|Pavilion of the Gods]], Aratis's shrine is newly established, cramped and spare. Of her true disciples, Aratis demands truth, honesty and forthrightness; hospitality, freely given to all who ask for it; and adherence to strict rules of diet and dress.

## Connections

- His closest kin is [[workshop/stonetop/places/Stonetop (village)#Residents\|Terrance]], his apprentice, whom he has adopted.
- [[workshop/stonetop/characters/Eirian the Lightbearer\|Eirian]] has stood beside him in battle against unnatural chaos.
- None of the others is a true disciple of Aratis.
- He has passed judgement on [[workshop/stonetop/characters/Nitza the Ranger\|Nitza]] and [[workshop/stonetop/characters/Ozbeg the Seeker\|Ozbeg]], and on Eirian, whom he did not find wanting.
